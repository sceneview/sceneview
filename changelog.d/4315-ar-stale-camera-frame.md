- **Android AR: `ARSceneView` follows the host activity, and the camera stays live while an AR screen animates in and out.** `ARSceneView` bound its session to `LocalLifecycleOwner`, which inside a `NavHost` is the destination: it leaves `RESUMED` when the exit transition starts, so the camera stopped and its last image stayed frozen on screen until the transition ended. The `lifecycle` parameter now defaults to the new `rememberHostLifecycle()`, the **host activity's** lifecycle: the session runs while the composable is in the composition and the activity is resumed, pauses when the activity pauses, and is closed on disposal. The camera is also live during the enter transition and under a dialog destination or a bottom sheet. **Breaking:** the camera now stays on wherever the host keeps an `ARSceneView` composed but not visible. In a Fragment kept off screen (a `ViewPager2` page) or on a screen covered by a full-screen `dialog()` destination, pass `lifecycle = LocalLifecycleOwner.current.lifecycle` to get the previous behaviour; with two AR pages left on the default, the last one to register takes the camera. A fragment hidden with `hide()` and a Compose `HorizontalPager` page have no lifecycle of their own: take the `ARSceneView` out of the composition there. When two `ARSceneView`s are composed together (one AR screen animating into another), the one that resumes last takes the camera and hands it back if it is disposed or paused first; a session resume that fails is now reported through `onSessionFailed` instead of crashing the lifecycle observer. Known limit: on a predictive back gesture between two AR screens, the screen under the finger is frozen until the gesture ends. 3D `SceneView` is unchanged.
