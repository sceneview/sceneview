- **Android AR: the camera stays live while an AR screen animates in and out.** `ARSceneView` bound its session to `LocalLifecycleOwner`, which inside a `NavHost` is the destination: it leaves `RESUMED` when the exit transition starts, so the camera stopped and its last image stayed frozen on screen until the transition ended. The `lifecycle` parameter now defaults to the **host activity's** lifecycle: the session runs while the composable is in the composition and the activity is resumed, pauses when the activity pauses, and is closed on disposal. The camera is also live during the enter transition and under a dialog destination or a bottom sheet. **Behaviour change:** to pause the camera while an `ARSceneView` stays composed but hidden (an off-screen pager page), pass that narrower lifecycle explicitly, e.g. `lifecycle = LocalLifecycleOwner.current.lifecycle`. When two `ARSceneView`s are composed together (one AR screen animating into another), the one that resumes last takes the camera and hands it back if it is disposed first. 3D `SceneView` is unchanged.
