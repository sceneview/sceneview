- **iOS: `ContactShadowNode`, a procedural contact shadow (Android `ContactShadowNode` parity).** The new `ContactShadowContext` presets (`floor`, `wall`, `tableTop`) use the same values as Android. The node lies on a horizontal or vertical quad, and its `intensity` can be changed live. `ContactShadowNode.under(model)` and `ModelNode.withContactShadow()` size the shadow from the model's bounds and rest it at the model's lowest point. `SceneView`'s automatic framing leaves the shadow out. In AR, `ARSceneView.placementShadow(_:)` gives tap-placed content one of three shadows: `.grounding` (the default, unchanged), `.contact(context, intensity:)` or `.none`.
