- **The Rerun demo now opens on a real room rebuilt in 3D, and is featured on the home screen ([#3993](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3993)).** It used to open on a camera screen that needed ARCore and a computer, and it read like a GPS tracker. It now plays a room recorded on a phone, bundled with the app so it works on every device and on the emulator. You see the camera's path as a ribbon, its photos in their frustums, the floor and table textured with the room, a coloured point cloud and two placed models, all orbitable. The room fades in once it is drawn, never on an empty stage, and the crane-down intro starts then. `sceneview://demo/ar-rerun` opens it directly. A filmstrip of the recorded frames scrubs the 3D view, with play/pause. A glass HUD shows tracking, the replay clock, the frame rate and the path, plane, point and anchor counts, and each figure toggles its layer. There are three views: 3D, a top-down Map and Camera, which shows the frames with the 3D view in a corner card. Live AR still records and streams your own session as before.
