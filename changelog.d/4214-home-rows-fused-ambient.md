- **The demo Home shows each demo's picture edge to edge, on Android and iOS ([#4214](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4214)).** The grey list with a small cropped picture is gone. The Featured demos are wide banners, and every other demo is a card whose picture fills its leading half and fades into a background tinted from the picture itself: deep in dark mode, pale in light mode, and always readable. Both apps now use the same pictures for the same demos. On iOS, the header also turns to Liquid Glass once the list scrolls under it (iOS 26; frosted glass before), and the titles underneath never show through the wordmark.
