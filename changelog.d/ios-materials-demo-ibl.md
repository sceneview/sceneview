- **ios-demo**: the Materials demo now renders with an image-based light (`.environment(.studio)`). It showcases `KHR_materials_transmission` / `_iridescence` / `_sheen`, and each of those is defined by what it does to the surrounding light — transmission refracts the environment, iridescence and sheen shift with the reflected view angle. With no IBL there was nothing to refract or reflect, so the curated models rendered as flat silhouettes and the demo hid its own subject. Same `.studio` preset `ModelViewerDemo` already used (#2114); verified on the simulator (specular highlights appear on the iridescent-beetle model where the pre-fix capture had none).
