- **`ViewNode` can face the camera, the way `TextNode` and `BillboardNode` do ([#4387](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4387)).** It takes the same `cameraPositionProvider: (() -> Position)? = null` — on the composable and on the three node constructors — with the same meaning: the front of the quad points at the position you return and its top edge stays toward world `+Y` (yaw and pitch, never roll). `rotation` is ignored while it is set and applies again when it goes back to `null`. The three nodes now share one implementation, which changes `BillboardNode` and `TextNode` too: the provider is a settable `cameraPositionProvider` property and follows recomposition (it was frozen at construction, so toggling it meant rebuilding the node); a billboard under a moving or rotating parent now keeps facing the camera (it only reacted to camera moves); and a camera straight above or below no longer produces a `NaN` transform that made the node vanish. Binary change: the `ViewNode` constructors and the `ViewNode` composable gain a parameter — recompile. The 2D in 3D demo's Inspect card has a "Face camera" switch.
