- **Android demo: Room Scan rebuilds the room on glass while it scans ([#4405](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4405)).** A scan in progress put a "Scanning" bar and an opaque 3D panel over the camera and left it a quarter of the screen. The camera is the screen now: one small pill (the clock and the point count) and a see-through 3D card share a row under the header, and a tap on the card enlarges it. More than half of the screen's height stays clear, against 23 % before.
- **Android demo: Room Scan says when a scan is full ([#4405](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4405)).** A long scan stopped counting at 500,000 points and said nothing. The count now reads against its limit as it comes close ("412k / 500k"), then "500k · full" with one line under it: new areas add no more points.
- **Android demo: Room Scan counts the surface it found, in m² ([#4405](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4405)).** "Surfaces" was a count of detected patches that fell every time two patches of one floor merged (0, 1, 4, 1, 6 over one room). The settings sheet now reads the area found.
