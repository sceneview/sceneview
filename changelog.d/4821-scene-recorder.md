- **`SceneRecorder`: shareable photos and videos of the scene, in AR too.** `rememberSceneRecorder()` (`io.github.sceneview.capture`) captures exactly what the scene renders, never the Compose UI and with no MediaProjection consent: `savePhoto()` writes a full-resolution `PixelCopy` as JPEG or PNG, and `startRecording()` / `stopRecording()` write an H.264 MP4 at 1080p and 30 fps by default, with an optional microphone track and an optional time limit. On `ARSceneView` the camera feed is in the file. A `CaptureOverlay` draws a watermark on every photo and video frame (video from Android 10). Pass `recorder.surfaceMirrorer` to `SceneView` or `ARSceneView`. A recording finalises its file and calls `onRecordingFinished` when it is stopped, reaches its limit, when the scene feeding it leaves composition, and, with `rememberSceneRecorder()`, when the screen goes to the background (`ON_STOP`). A busy microphone records the video silent instead of failing. `SurfaceMirrorer` now wakes an idle on-demand scene when a mirror is added. The Android demo's shared Record action moves onto it and gains Take photo, a share action and a one-minute limit, also on AR Finger Drawing.
