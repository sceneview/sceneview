- **Android demo: Rerun planes no longer shimmer in the replay.** Every upward plane, table tops and beds included, was flattened onto the floor photo, and overlapping ARCore patches sat at the same depth, so the photos z-fought as the camera orbited. Each plane is now drawn at its own depth: floor patches under the grid a step apart, table tops at their real height, walls stepped towards the room, outlines in front of their fills. The dollhouse's opaque fills use the same layering.
- **arsceneview: `PlaneRenderer` no longer blinks planes ARCore updates.** A plane that was updated kept the visibility it had when it was created, while every other plane took the pass's, so in the default "render center" mode a plane updated every other frame flickered. Merged (subsumed) planes also recreated their visualizer on every update and piled up material instances (`||` instead of `&&`).
- **Android demo: the Rerun replay draws the room's floor-plan dimensions.** The room found by the scan, squared to its walls, is measured and drawn on the floor like an architect's plan (dimension lines, extension lines, figures) on the two sides facing you, and the HUD names it (`Room 3.4 × 4.1 m · 14 m²`).
