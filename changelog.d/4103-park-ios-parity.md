- **Demo app: the Park is a park, on Android and iOS ([#4103](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4103)).** It used to show four tree scans at unrelated sizes in a grey studio under a softbox. It now shows a pair of oaks, a bench, a street lamp and a fern at one consistent scale, standing on a lawn in Poly Haven's "Chinese Garden". The four models weigh 12.5 MB instead of 63 MB, so the scene loads about five times faster. On iOS, models now stand on the ground (before, a model's position replaced the offset that grounds it), and leaves are cut out instead of drawn as pale translucent cards: RealityKit read their opacity from the wrong texture channel. The Nile statue, which no screen has shown since the Scene Gallery left Android, is no longer credited there. The iOS viewer's environments are named after what they show, as on Android: "Seascape" instead of "Sunset", "Interior" and "Studio" instead of "Studio" and "Studio Warm". "Chinese Garden" is added. On iOS the Credits sheet groups the Scene Gallery models under "Scene Gallery".
