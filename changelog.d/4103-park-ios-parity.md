- **Demo app: the Park is a park, on Android and iOS ([#4103](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4103)).** It used to show four tree scans at unrelated sizes in a grey studio under a softbox. It now shows a pair of oaks, a bench, a street lamp and a fern at one consistent scale, standing on a lawn in Poly Haven's "Chinese Garden". The four models are a 12.5 MB download instead of 63 MB. The lawn renders the same natural green on both platforms. On iOS, models now stand on the ground (before, a model's position replaced the offset that grounds it), the screen is titled "Park Scene" as on Android, the camera frames the whole park even when the oaks stream in last (without ever snapping back a camera the user has orbited or zoomed), and the leaves of streamed models are cut out instead of drawn as pale translucent cards: RealityKit read their opacity from the wrong texture channel. Offline on Android, the stand-in for the oaks now faces the camera. The Nile statue, which no screen has shown since the Scene Gallery left Android, is no longer credited there. The iOS viewer's environments are named after what they show, like Android's: "Studio" and "Interior" instead of "Studio Warm" and "Studio", and "Seascape" instead of "Sunset" for the cloudy sky over the sea (an iOS-only tile: Android's "Sunset" is a different HDR). "Chinese Garden" is added. As on Android, it comes first and is the environment of a first launch, and Studio comes before Interior. On iOS the Credits sheet groups the Scene Gallery models under "Scene Gallery".
