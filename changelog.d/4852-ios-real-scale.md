- **iOS: real-world size placement and metric dimensions ([#4852](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4852)).** `ARPlacementController.setModel(_:ticket:size:)` takes a `PlacementSize`: `.realWorld` keeps the authored metres (a 1.8 m figure stands 1.8 m tall), `.fitInto(meters:)` fits the longest side into a preview size. The controller publishes `placementSize` and `baseSize`, and exposes `placedSize` (follows the pinch) and `isAtRealWorldSize`; the model's bottom centre stays on the surface. `ModelNode.worldSize` and `ModelNode.worldBoundingBox` report world-space dimensions. The iOS demo shows "Actual size · 100% · 1.80 m tall" / "Preview size · 100%", a reset-to-100% button, and W × D × H in the View in 3D sheet. The existing `setModel(_:ticket:previewSize:)` is unchanged.
