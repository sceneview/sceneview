- **iOS demo — Museum & Space:** the Model Viewer's picker gets a "Museum & Space" section with four HD pack models, at parity with Android: the Smithsonian **Apollo 11 Command Module** and **Apollo 11 Interior** (CC0), the Smithsonian **Woolly Mammoth** skeleton (CC0) and NASA's **Perseverance Rover** (NASA media guidelines, insignia removed). None ships in the App Store build, and none downloads on its own: the HD pack now downloads per model. Only the Flight Helmet (52 MB) is still prefetched on Wi-Fi. A Museum model shows its thumbnail and a pill with its own size ("Apollo 11 Interior · download 38 MB"). A tap opens a dialog that names the model, states its size and, on cellular, warns that it uses mobile data. Once downloaded, the model appears at its real-world size, read from a new optional `scale` field of the shared `assets/hd-pack/ios.json` manifest. Perseverance opens still: its 23 mechanism clips stay under Animate, paused. These models are not offered in AR yet. Their credits appear in the app's Credits screen, read from the manifest. The About row now covers the Flight Helmet plus the models you downloaded. A slow HD load no longer lands over a model picked after it. A demo header whose status pill is too long for one row puts the pill on a second row instead of squeezing the demo title to a single letter, and keeps that layout while the download counts up.
