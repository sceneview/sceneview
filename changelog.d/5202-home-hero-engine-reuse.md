- **Demo: returning to Home no longer builds a new engine for its 3D hero ([#5202](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5202)).** The hero renders into a `TextureView`, and on Adreno 618 (Pixel 4a) every new EGL context that draws into one leaves a GPU driver context behind; after about 200 returns to Home the app could no longer create an engine and closed. The hero's engine now lives above the navigation and is reused on every visit, while everything made with it is still released when Home leaves. The hero's Filmic view also frees its two colour gradings with it instead of leaving them in the engine.
