- **Android demo: the Rerun sample is now "Room Scan", and its cameras fit the room into the part of the screen the controls leave free ([#4306](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4306)).**
  The 3D view frames the box of what the scan drew (path, planes and anchors whole; the two point clouds trimmed of their strays) between the figures and the timeline, in portrait and landscape, from as far back as the fit asks — tested up to a 20 × 9 m box, upright and on its side, in 3D and on the map, which the former 40 m stop cropped. That box is what ARCore reported: the room's size itself can over-read on a real scan ([#4328](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4328)). Left alone the view sways gently in front of the room instead of turning all the way round it; the map squares itself with the walls; a pinch, a drag or a pan can no longer put the camera inside the cloud, under the floor or far away from the room; automatic moves are eased and take the shortest way round.
  "Build 3D model" no longer opens a second screen: a full-width **Points | Surface** switch heads the timeline card and draws a preview of the room's surface in the same view, under the same camera, with the `.glb` share button beside it. The preview is still there after a look at the Camera view — its model is parsed once and kept for the replay, not read again at each return — and it is built once, however often the switch is flipped while it builds. When there is no surface to show, the card says why (a scan too thin, not enough memory, or a model the view could not load) instead of blaming the scan every time.
  The tracking aids (trail, frusta, live points) are toned down so the room reads first, and a phone on its side gets its own layout: figures and timeline on either side of the room, the timeline on one row, no card over the room.
  The demo id (`ar-rerun`), its deep link and its mode keys are unchanged.
