- **iOS demo: a Rerun `.rrd` export now opens again with everything the session had ([#4093](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4093)).** This brings the Android fix of [#4080](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4080) to iOS. The live feature points are written as a timed `world/points/live` entity, so a reopened file shows the map growing and the live points at the times they were seen, instead of a point counter with nothing drawn. Plane photos keep their transparent texels (RGBA textures) instead of coming back black. Every photo of the session is exported, not only the keyframes. A file without live points, such as an older export, still opens with the whole map shown from the first frame.
