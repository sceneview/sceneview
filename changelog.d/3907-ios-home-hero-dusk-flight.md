- **iOS demo: the home header is the same live dusk flight as on Android ([#3907](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3907)).** The rounded card with a studio-lit helmet is gone: a full-bleed stage now runs from the top of the display, with the same valley of flat-shaded ridges streaming under a swaying, rolling camera, the low sun, the fog that dissolves the far ridges into the dusk sky, and the Model Viewer's helmet turning beside the camera. It uses the same flight path, easing, entrance, tilt response, parallax and scrim as Android. The header reads white over the sky and returns to the page colours once scrolled. Once scrolled away the 3D view goes idle, and under Reduce Motion it stops animating once the frame is posed.
