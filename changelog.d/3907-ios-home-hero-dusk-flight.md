- **iOS demo: the home header is the same live dusk flight as on Android ([#3907](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3907)).** The rounded card with a studio-lit helmet is gone: a full-bleed stage now runs from the top of the display, with the same valley of flat-shaded ridges streaming under a swaying, rolling camera, the low sun, the fog that dissolves the far ridges into the dusk sky, and the Model Viewer's helmet turning beside the camera. It uses the same flight path, easing, entrance, tilt response, parallax and scrim as Android. The header reads white over the sky and returns to the page colours once scrolled.
- **iOS demo: the home hero builds its renderer once and sleeps when unseen.** Scrolling no longer recreates the scene renderer on every frame (1,628 builds over one scroll before, 1 after); the 3D view unmounts once the hero is scrolled away (app CPU ~20% → 0%) and resumes on the same frame, the per-frame callback stops under Reduce Motion once the frame is posed, the terrain fog is shaded off the main thread, and the hero copy presses like every other card.
