- **HDR environments are lit on every screen again with Filament 1.77 ([#4935](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4935)).** Since Filament 1.77.0, `IBLPrefilterContext` creates its entities in a second copy of Filament's entity manager, and any HDR environment loaded after the first engine was destroyed came out black: black skybox, no image-based lighting. SceneView now reserves the first 1024 entity slots when it loads Filament, before any entity exists, which keeps the prefilter working on every engine. The limit: after 512 `EnvironmentLoader`s have been destroyed in one process, a new HDR environment is black again, until Filament fixes the split upstream. An app that creates Filament entities before SceneView loads Filament gets a warning in logcat.
