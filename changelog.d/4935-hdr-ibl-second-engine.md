- **HDR environments are lit on every screen again with Filament 1.77 ([#4935](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4935)).** Since Filament 1.77.0, `IBLPrefilterContext` creates its entities in a second copy of Filament's entity manager, and any HDR environment loaded after the first engine was destroyed came out black: black skybox, no image-based lighting. SceneView now reserves the first 4096 entity slots when it loads Filament, before any entity exists (about 0.5 ms), which keeps the prefilter working on every engine. The limit: once 2048 `EnvironmentLoader`s have prefiltered an HDR in one process, alive or destroyed, the next HDR environment is black again, until Filament fixes the split upstream. An app that creates Filament entities before SceneView loads Filament gets a warning in logcat.
