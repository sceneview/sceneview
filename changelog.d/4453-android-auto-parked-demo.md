- **Android Auto sample: SceneView Drive** — `samples/android-auto-demo` shows SceneView as an Android Auto parked app. "Night Garage" puts a car on a turntable with orbit, zoom, and car, finish and lighting switches sized for a car screen; "Drive" takes that car onto the garage floor with touch steering, throttle and brake, a chase camera, and headlights in the Night lighting. It is a plain activity declared with `android:appCategory="game"` and `CAR_LAUNCHER`, no Car App Library, with an R8 release build that can be uploaded to Play. Proven on an emulator at head-unit sizes, not yet in a car (#4453).
