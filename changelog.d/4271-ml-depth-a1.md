- **arsceneview**: `ArDepthFrame` (metric depth in millimetres, with confidence, intrinsics, camera pose and a `source`: `ArDepthFrame.Source.Native` or `Ml`, the same cases as iOS `ARDepthFrame.Source`) and `MlDepthSession`. The session turns any `MonocularDepthEstimator` into metric depth frames: it runs the network off the render thread at up to 5 Hz, scales its relative inverse depth to metres with a robust fit (`1/z = s·d + t`) on ARCore plane samples and feature points, smooths the scale over time, pauses when the phone gets hot, and gives up only after three failed runs in a row. It publishes on a `StateFlow`. Feed it from `onSessionUpdated`.
- **arsceneview-depth-ml** (new module, not published yet): `DepthAnythingV2Estimator` runs Depth Anything V2 **Small** (Apache-2.0) on LiteRT, on CPU or GPU. The 27.7 MB model is downloaded on first use from a pinned revision and checked against its SHA-256, so the APK carries none of it; concurrent loads share one download. LiteRT is pinned to 2.1.5 (single AAR, no `android.uniquePackageNames` override).
- **Android demo**: the Depth Visualization demo gains an **ARCore | ML** pill. ML mode shows the estimate in metres on the same false-colour ramp, with a status card (download, waiting for surfaces, ms per estimate, Hz, anchors, fit error). It also works on devices without ARCore's Depth API, and switches ARCore's own depth off while ML is shown. The debug (universal) APK now ships only `arm64-v8a` and `x86_64` native libraries.
