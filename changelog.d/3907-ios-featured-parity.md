- **iOS demo app: Materials and Lighting now open on the same scene as Android.** Materials shows Android's wall of nine labelled spheres: chrome, gold, copper, aluminum, ceramic, car paint with a clear coat, velvet with sheen, crystal and a neon glow. The camera slowly sweeps across them, and you tap a sphere to zoom in on it and tune its metallic and roughness. It needs no network and no Sketchfab key, so the "Mosquito in Amber" offline stand-in is gone. The crystal sphere is labelled "transparency" on iPhone because RealityKit has no refraction. Lighting now stands the Damaged Helmet on a slate floor with the chrome and matte probe balls at its feet, framed on the helmet rather than on the floor, and circles it slowly (Animate pauses the orbit). The Home screen no longer ranks Cosmos and Augmented Faces at the same position. Light and dark.
