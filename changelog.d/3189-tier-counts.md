- **The tool-claim gate now derives the tier split, and refuses rather than narrowing itself in silence ([#3189](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3189)).** `tools/check-mcp-tool-claims.js` compared every "N tools" claim against the stdio count, the mounted total and the per-package counts — but not the **free/Pro split**, which is legitimate to advertise and derivable from neither. Measured: the stdio package defines 31 tools of which three are Pro (`render_3d_preview`, `create_3d_artifact`, `generate_scene`), so its free surface is 28; adding the gateway-only-but-free `view_3d_model` gives 29 free against 38 Pro, totalling the 67 mounted. `mcp/mcpize.yaml` advertises "28 free tools" and is **right** — the gate would have rejected it and pushed a reader to break a true claim, which is worse than missing a false one. The split is now parsed from `mcp/src/tiers.ts` and folded into the accepted set. The first attempt at that parse assumed the arrays close with `];` when they close with `] as const;`, matched nothing, and dropped the counts with **no error at all** — so the gate now exits 2 on an unparseable tier map, the same contract it already had for the registry and the sample count. Self-test 12 → 14 cases; one of the two new ones was found vacuous under mutation (its fixture reached the assertion through `free - 1` rather than the Pro count) and was rewritten with a distinct Pro count before shipping.
