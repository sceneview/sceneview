- **Demo app: Pool in the garden, a real-size pool dug into your lawn ([#4571](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4571)).** A new AR demo places an in-ground pool on your lawn at its real size — 6 × 3, 8 × 4 or 10 × 5 m, 1.2 to 1.5 m deep — and the grass stays in front of it everywhere but the opening, so it looks dug into the ground. Clear water with moving ripples and light patterns on the bottom, a stone edge, Roman steps, four liner colours, and a night mode: the garden goes dark and two underwater lamps light the pool. Drag to move it, twist to turn it; pinching never changes its size.
- **`arsceneview`: dig content into the real ground, and keep a placed object at real size ([#4571](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4571)).** `ARCameraStream.isOccluderMeshEnabled` draws the camera feed first, so a mesh using `MaterialLoader.createOcclusionInstance()`, ordered with `ARCameraStream.OCCLUDER_PRIORITY`, keeps the real world on screen in front of content modelled below the ground or behind a wall. `AutoPlacementState.allowsScaling = false` makes the pinch, `scaleTo` and the double-tap leave a placed object at its real size while drag and twist still move and turn it. Both default to the previous behaviour.
