<!-- category: Changed -->
- **A model now looks like a product shot out of the box, in 3D and in AR.** `RenderQuality.Default`, which `SceneView` applies unless told otherwise, now renders soft shadows (`ShadowType.DPCF`) instead of hard ones, SSAO at MEDIUM quality with bilateral upsampling instead of Filament's blotchy LOW default, and the default main light comes from the front, upper right (`DEFAULT_MAIN_LIGHT_DIRECTION`) instead of straight down, so the front of a model is lit instead of left in the fill light. The cost stays mid-range: no MSAA, MEDIUM HDR buffer, fixed resolution. `RenderQuality.Cinematic` moves to contact-hardening shadows (`ShadowType.PCSS`) and HIGH SSAO upsampling. In AR, `createARView` gets the same soft shadows, `PlacementScene` now casts a ground shadow under placed models by default (`groundShadows = true`, like `AutoPlacementScene`), and passing any `renderQuality` to `ARSceneView` no longer turns SSAO and bloom on over the camera feed. Opt out with `renderQuality = RenderQuality.Performance`, your own `mainLightNode`, `view.setShadowType(View.ShadowType.PCF)`, or `PlacementScene(groundShadows = false)`.
