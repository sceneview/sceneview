- **A model now looks like a product shot out of the box, in 3D and in AR.** `createView` now gives every view soft shadows (`ShadowType.DPCF`) instead of hard ones and SSAO at MEDIUM quality with bilateral upsampling instead of Filament's blotchy LOW default; `RenderQuality.Default`, which `SceneView` applies unless told otherwise, leaves those three settings as they are, so your own `view.setShadowType(...)` survives it, and the default main light is tilted slightly to the right and front (`DEFAULT_MAIN_LIGHT_DIRECTION`) instead of pointing straight down, so the front of a model catches some key light while its shadow still pools under it. The cost stays mid-range: no MSAA, MEDIUM HDR buffer, fixed resolution. `RenderQuality.Cinematic` moves from `ShadowType.PCF` to contact-hardening shadows (`ShadowType.PCSS`) and HIGH SSAO upsampling. In AR, `createARView` gets the same soft shadows, `PlacementScene`'s `groundShadows` parameter now defaults to `true` (it was `false`), so placed models cast a ground shadow out of the box, like `AutoPlacementScene`, and passing any `renderQuality` to `ARSceneView` no longer turns SSAO and bloom on over the camera feed. Opt out with `renderQuality = RenderQuality.Performance`, your own `mainLightNode`, `view.setShadowType(View.ShadowType.PCF)`, or `PlacementScene(groundShadows = false)`.
