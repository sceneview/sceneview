- **Point & Ask answers stay on screen and no longer cover each other ([#4071](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4071)).** An answer pinned near the edge of the screen used to run off it, and answers pinned close together were drawn on top of each other. Every frame, the cards are now laid out inside the safe area, clear of the system bars and the demo's controls, and kept apart. The newest answer stays over the object it describes, and older ones slide aside. An answer that has no room is hidden until there is room again. Cards still belong to their place in the room: once there is space, each one slides back over its object.
