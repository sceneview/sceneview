- **Android demo: the Rerun dollhouse shows your room in the colours your phone saw, not a path and white markers.** A scan that kept its dense coloured cloud stands as that cloud, cut open above the floor and trimmed of what the camera saw through doorways. An older scan shows its photographed surfaces and its points in their captured colours. The path you walked is hidden by default; a switch in the settings shows it.
- **Android demo: Real size puts the room's floor on your floor.** When ARCore found no floor plane, the dollhouse guessed the floor from the phone's starting height and the room floated above or sank below the real one. It now finds the floor in the dense cloud. At real size the plinth is gone, so the floor sits on the surface the room was placed on, at 1:1.
