- **Demo app — Cosmos:** a new demo of four procedural, real-time space scenes lit only by their own HDR light and bloom: a barred spiral galaxy of ~40,000 stars, a plasma star with a noise-shaded surface and magnetic loops, a looping burst of charged-particle tracks, and a vortex flow field with travelling pulses. Pick a scene from the dock or let the tour cycle them. Three new demo materials (`cosmos_sprite`, `cosmos_ribbon`, `cosmos_plasma`) show how to draw glowing points and strokes as camera-facing geometry, because `POINTS` and `LINES` draw one pixel wide on mobile.
