- **Demo app — Cosmos:** a new demo of four procedural, real-time space scenes lit only by their own HDR light and bloom: a four-arm spiral galaxy of 60,000 stars with dark dust lanes, on screen within a second of opening, a plasma star with a noise-shaded surface and magnetic loops, a looping burst of charged-particle tracks, and a vortex flow field with travelling pulses. Pick a scene from the dock or let the tour cycle them. Four new demo materials (`cosmos_sprite`, `cosmos_ribbon`, `cosmos_plasma`, `cosmos_dust`) show how to draw glowing points and strokes as camera-facing geometry, because `POINTS` and `LINES` draw one pixel wide on mobile.
