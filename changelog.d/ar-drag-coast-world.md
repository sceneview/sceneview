- **An AR object flicked across the floor no longer follows the camera.** After a drag is released at speed, the object keeps sliding. Until now that slide moved a point across the screen and hit-tested under it every frame, so turning the phone mid-slide dragged the object along with the camera. The slide now travels along the surface the object was dragged on, in world space, and still stops where the surface ends or once it leaves the view. Applies to every editable AR node (`AnchorNode`, `PoseNode`) and to `AutoPlacementScene` objects.
