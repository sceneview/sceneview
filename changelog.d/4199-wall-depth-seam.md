- **Wall placement now finds plain painted walls ([#4070](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4070)).** A bare wall often never shows up as a detected surface, so wall placement could search forever. It now also accepts a wall hit just past the edge of a detected wall. On phones that can measure depth, it accepts any point the camera sees as upright. On any phone, it can also find the wall where the detected floor stops. `AutoPlacementResult` and `AutoPlacementCandidate` gain `planeOrNull` for a wall found this way.
