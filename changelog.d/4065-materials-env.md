- **Demo: the Materials backdrop is smooth and the neon sign glows in colour ([#4065](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4065)).** The studio background broke every bright edge into stair-stepped blocks: it was a 256 px cube of an HDR whose light panels are far brighter than white. The demo now builds its own backdrop from the same HDR, with softened highlights and a slight depth-of-field blur, at twice the resolution. The lighting on the spheres is unchanged. The "Neon sign" sphere rendered plain white because a pale tint at four times strength clips to white. It now glows blue: `Primary` at 1.5 times strength.
