- **The web demo is the place to try SceneView in a browser.** It opens on a KitchenAid stand mixer in a photo studio that follows light and dark mode, with a Material 3 layout: a navigation rail and side sheet on desktop, the 3D view first and a chip row over a bottom sheet on phones. The curated catalog drops the Khronos samples and low-poly models for 15 realistic, openly licensed models with thumbnails and on-screen credits, fetched from `assets-v1` like the other demos. A "Use it in your app" button leads to the web quick start, and the page reports model, tab, AR and call-to-action events through the site's analytics.
