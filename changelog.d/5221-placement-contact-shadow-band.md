- AR: the contact shadow under a placed object now shows around it. The object hides its own footprint, so the shadow a user sees is the band just outside it; that band was too pale and missing at the corners and along most of a sofa's edge. `AutoPlacementModel` now draws a rounded rectangle on the footprint, 0.4 opacity at the object's edge and faded out 12 cm beyond it on every side. `ContactShadowNode` gains an optional `footprint` (`ContactShadowFootprint`) for that shape; without one it draws the same ellipse as before ([#5221](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/5221)).
