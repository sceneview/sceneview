- **iOS: keyframed camera paths and a Camera Path demo.** SceneViewSwift gains `CameraPose`, `CameraTimeline` and `CameraPosePath`, with the same sampling rules as Android: sorted keys, clamp or loop, capped Catmull-Rom tangents, short-way slerp, per-key or whole-path easing and `lookAt`. The `orbit(bounds:)`, `travelling(from:distance:)` and `dolly(target:from:toDistance:)` presets build ready-made shots. `CameraPathPlayer` plays, pauses, seeks, loops and changes speed on wall-clock time. `SceneCameraPose(_:focusDistance:)` and `CameraControls.follow(_:focusDistance:)` drive SceneView's orbit camera from any pose. The iOS demo adds `camera-path`: three subjects, Orbit / Travelling / Dolly shots and a glass timeline bar with play/pause, scrubbing and a clock.
