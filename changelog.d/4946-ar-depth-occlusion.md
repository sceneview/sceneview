- **AR depth occlusion no longer flickers or cuts models on the floor.** ARCore depth now goes through a temporal filter: a swing is only accepted once the next image confirms it, and short holes keep the last depth. A real surface must also be nearer than the model by a tolerance that grows with distance. Occlusion fades out between 5 m and 8 m, so a distant model is never chopped. Shadows inherit the same behaviour. To log the hole share and depth lag, run `adb shell setprop log.tag.SceneViewDepth DEBUG`. Custom `depthOcclusionMaterialFile` copies of the old material should be dropped.
