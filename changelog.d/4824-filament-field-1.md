- **`ModelLoader` refuses the gltfio calls that aborted the app ([#4824](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4824)).** A failed Filament precondition aborts the whole process from inside the JNI, out of reach of any `try`. `updateLoad()`, `progress` and `isLoading` now do nothing once the loader or its `Engine` is destroyed, so a frame that runs after a dispose no longer reads freed memory. A destroyed loader, or one whose `Engine` is gone, throws an `IllegalStateException` from `createModel`, `createInstancedModel` and `createInstance` instead of crashing; `destroy()` can be called twice. A model with a skin of more than 256 joints, or a texture larger than the GPU supports, is refused like any other model that cannot load (an `IllegalArgumentException`, or `null` from `loadModel`) instead of aborting inside `createAsset` or `asyncBeginLoad`.
