- **iOS automatic placement now feels like ARMV, the SceneView Android AR model viewer app ([#4863](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4863)).** On `AutoPlacementScene` / `ARPlacementController`, the model eases toward the finger during a drag (18/s), a thrown drag glides on (released faster than 150 px/s, τ 0.35 s) and a flicked twist keeps spinning (faster than 45°/s); any new touch stops both. A double tap anywhere toggles between 100 % and the user's own size over 260 ms, also as `toggleScale()`. New `recenter()` brings the model back in front of the user, onto the floor along the centre ray, turned with the camera's bearing, keeping pinch and twist. A first placement grows in from 1 %. With instant placement, after 6 unresolved anchors the recovery is now abandoned: the model stays where it stands and no timer retries it (the 3 s cooldown is gone); another plane, the plane growing by 25 % or the user moving the model re-arms landing.
