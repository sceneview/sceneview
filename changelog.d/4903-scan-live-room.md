- **3D Scan scans a room in first person ([#4903](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4903)).** The camera fills the screen with the capture painted on it, see-through so the room stays readable. A chevron at the screen's edge, clear of the status card and the controls, points to the largest gap, or to the part being fixed or reviewed, a ribbon on the floor leads there, a light tick is felt each time the phone lines up, a small north-up model of the room shows where the phone stands and the floor to scan again in red, and one short message shows at a time (Tracking lost, Too dark, Slow down, Move closer, Scan full), in a place that stays put. Fix now's fill line gives way to that message, says what to try when the part stops filling, and its amber frame stays under the chrome. The live map pane, the 3D card, the dock, the Turn left / right arrows and the saving orbit are gone from the room capture. The scope chooser offers Object and Room; older Home and Area scans still open.
