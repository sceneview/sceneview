- **Android demo app: usage statistics, crash reports and "new sample" notifications ([#4244](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4244)).** The SceneView Demo app now reports usage statistics, never linked to your identity (which samples are opened, how long they are used, where the AR flow stops) and crashes through Firebase, and can notify you when new samples land; tapping a notification opens that sample. Both can be turned off in **About → Privacy & notifications**, and the app asks for notification permission only after you have tried a few samples, never on first launch. Nothing is used for advertising. The SceneView libraries themselves still ship no telemetry, and a build without a Firebase config behaves exactly as before.
