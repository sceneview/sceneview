- **AR: Plane Renderer V2 draws detected surfaces as soft dots ([#3507](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3507)).** The V2 plane look is now a fine dot pattern anchored in the room, so it stays still while you move. It fades at the plane edges and with distance, and each new or growing plane ripples in. Floors, ceilings and walls each get their own subtle tint. A soft spot and ring mark the surface under the screen centre, and the dots fade out once an object is placed. It allocates nothing per frame. The Plane Renderer V2 demo now says in one line what you are looking at, and lets you place and pick up a toy car. `PlaneRendererV2` now draws every tracked plane by default (`RENDER_ALL`, was `RENDER_CENTER`), and its metallic, roughness and texture parameters no longer change the look.
