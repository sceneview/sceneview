- **Demo app: opening Models from the Home hero no longer stalls ([#4143](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4143)).** When the user left Home before the hero finished loading, its engine kept loading (the HDR sky, the terrain material, the helmet textures, the terrain upload) and competed with the viewer for the GPU. On an emulator this held the viewer on "Loading model…" for many seconds and could raise "isn't responding". The hero now starts and loads only while Home is on screen, packs the terrain off the main thread, and `SceneRenderer` no longer waits a second time for a swap chain it has already released.
