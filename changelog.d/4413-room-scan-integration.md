- **Android demo: a replayed Room Scan sits whole inside the screen, its dimensions included ([#4413](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4413)).** The view was fitted to the walls alone, so the figures written outside them ("3.4 m") ran off the screen edge, and a thin wall could be cut off the frame. The fit now takes the figures in, and the Surface view is framed on the surface it shows instead of on the hidden point cloud, which left it small and off-centre.
- **Android demo: Room Scan opens on its glass controls, not on a black screen ([#4413](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4413)).** For about three seconds the screen was black with a greyed shutter and the old dock. It now shows the scan screen from the first frame and says "Starting camera…", then "Looking for the room…".
- **Android demo: Room Scan shows one point count ([#4413](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4413)).** The count shown while scanning included points seen only once, which a saved scan drops: one scan read 32k live and 5.6k once stored, and the 500k limit counted the larger figure. The live count, the saved scan and the limit now count the same points.
- **Android demo: Room Scan's size no longer shrinks and comes back ([#4413](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4413)).** A room read 3.7 × 3.9 m, then 2.4 × 2.3 m for two seconds, then 3.7 × 3.9 m again. A smaller reading is now held five seconds before it is shown.
