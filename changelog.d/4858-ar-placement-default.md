<!-- category: Changed -->
- **`AutoPlacementScene` places like a polished AR viewer by default ([#4858](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4858)).** The object lands on the first tracked surface, or, after `AutoPlacementState.estimatedFallbackMillis` (600 ms, `null` to wait for a surface) without one, on the estimated floor through ARCore instant placement, at `AutoPlacementState.placementDistance` ahead and facing the viewer; it settles onto the tracked spot once ARCore has it (`placementSource`, `isSettled`). It grows in from 1 % of its size, stands on a contact shadow and shows a flat ring around its footprint (dashed while estimated, closed once tracked, thicker under the fingers) that fades out after a moment. One finger drags it anywhere on screen, off a plane too (up to 5 m), and a flicked drag glides on; two fingers turn it and a flicked twist spins on; a touch stops both. A double tap anywhere toggles real size. `AutoPlacementState.recenter()` brings it back in front of the camera. An anchor ARCore refuses is retried with back-off and given up after six attempts instead of every frame.
- **Breaking: `AutoPlacementModel` shows the model at its authored size.** `scaleToUnits` now defaults to `null` (it was `0.3f`); pass `scaleToUnits = 0.3f` to keep the old preview size. The new `contactShadow` parameter (default `true`) turns the shadow off.
- **Breaking: the torus selection ring is replaced by the flat placement ring.** `SelectionRingNode`, `SelectionRing.tubeRadius`, `ALPHA`, `FADE_MS`, `LIFT`, `MAJOR_SEGMENTS` and `MINOR_SEGMENTS` are removed, and the ring no longer follows `isSelected`. `AutoPlacementState.showsSelectionRing` is deprecated in favour of `showsPlacementRing`. `PlacementRingNode`, `PlacementRingLook`, `PlacementRingVisibility` and `rememberPlacementRingVisible` let custom content draw the same ring.
- **Breaking: `AutoPlacementState.onFrame`'s `commit` takes the `PlacementSource` it commits.** A lambda literal still compiles; a `() -> Boolean` value is passed as `{ value() }`. `FrameInput` gains `estimatedAvailable` and `anchorSettled`, both defaulted.

<!-- category: Added -->
- **`PlacementFeel`, `PlacementMath` and `AnchorRetryPolicy`.** The constants and pure geometry behind the default placement (fallback delay, floor estimate, placement distance from the object's size, screen rays, glide and spin coasts), named like the iOS viewer's and covered by JVM tests.
- **Demo app: One-Call AR places the mixer straight away.** It lands on the estimated floor when no surface is found yet, glides and spins when flicked, and gains a Recenter button.
