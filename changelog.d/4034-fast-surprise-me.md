- **Demo app: "Surprise me" lands in a few seconds and never shows a broken model ([#4034](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4034)).** A roll used to search Sketchfab for "pbr" and take any hit up to 200,000 faces with no size cap, so it could pull a 20 MB room interior, fall through three searches on a failure, and show the model while its textures were still uploading, as scattered untextured blocks. It now draws from twelve curated single objects of the CC-BY sample registry (5.2 MB at most), in shuffle-bag order so no model repeats before the others have shown, and downloads the next one while you look at the current one. A download over 8 MB or 15 s is abandoned for a model already on disk, the previous model stays on screen until the new one is fully loaded and has a sane size, and picking a model while a roll is running cancels the roll. The model's author and licence are shown under the pill.
