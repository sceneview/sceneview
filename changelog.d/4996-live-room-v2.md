- **Live Room: the room appears while you scan it.** A room scan in the Android demo keeps the camera clear: the live 3D map of the coloured cloud builds up in a card under it, with the time, the floor scanned in m² and the points, and no walls, borders or chevrons. A pose ARCore teleports while it still reports tracking no longer fuses depth and photos in the wrong place: the scan holds until the pose comes back, and offers to finish or restart if it does not. Stop saves at once and names each save step, without the plane painting a room scan never used. The result opens on the room as a dollhouse: its floor and four walls as flat planes textured from the scan's photos, single-sided so the walls facing you open the room as you orbit, with the furniture as small coloured points inside. A room that is not a rectangle — an L, a T — gets its own walls and floor rather than the box round it: the scan's strong wall lines cut the box into cells, and a cell the scan never saw the floor of is left out, while anything in doubt stays in, so a rectangular room keeps exactly its box. Shells kept by an earlier version are built again. It is built in a few seconds and kept with the scan, so reopening it is instant; the Photo layer stays one tap away. A scan that does not stand as a room keeps the previous result, where the cleaned model replaces the raw scan only when it passes a quality check.
- **3D Scan keeps its raw depth, so the fusion can be replayed off the phone.** A depth scan in the Android demo now records every depth frame it fuses — ARCore's raw depth, its confidence, the colours read for it, the depth lens and the camera pose — and every frame the pose guard held back, flagged as such, streamed to a file on a background thread and kept beside the session as `capture-depth.bin`, with `depth_frame` and `pose_guard` lines in its log. Sessions without it open as before, and readers that do not know it ignore it. On the JVM, `ScanDepthReplay` runs the same fusion and the same pose guard over it and gives back the cloud the phone saved; `ScanDepthCorpusTest` replays a folder of sessions and compares the result with each saved cloud.
- **Live Room: the furniture is a surface, not dots.** When a room scan kept its raw depth, the dollhouse's furniture is fused again from it into a continuous, vertex-coloured, lit surface (a 2 cm TSDF and marching cubes, off the main thread) and cut by the same rules that picked the object points — inside the room, clear of the floor and walls — with small floating pieces dropped. It is part of the shell's `.glb`, double-sided, and shown instead of the points; a scan without its depth, or whose depth leaves no furniture surface, keeps the points. Shells kept by an earlier version are built again.
- **3D Scan: a scan with its raw depth and no room opens on its whole fused surface.** An object scan, an outside scan or a room scan that finds no room box, when it kept its depth recording, now opens its Model layer on the full surface fused again from that recording — floor included, small floating pieces dropped, holes up to 15 cm closed (a doorway or an unscanned part stays open), simplified past 250,000 triangles — built off the main thread with the same progress and cancel as the room shell, kept with the scan and read back on the next open; export writes it. The live TSDF is let go as soon as a readable recording is found, so the two never sit in memory together; without a recording the result is unchanged. The Photo layer stays one tap away.
- **3D Scan: a result that is being built says so.** Opening a scan whose surface is fused from its depth shows a progress bar that follows the real work (fusion, meshing, hole closing) from the first frame, and the surface appears as soon as it is ready instead of after its wire reveal.
- **3D Scan: sharper photos.** A scan now keeps every photo at the camera image's resolution, up to 640 px on the long side (they were 320 px), within a bounded budget of about 29 MB for the longest scan.
- **Object scans keep their captured depth range when reopened.** Rebuilding the surface uses the recording's range, including the Object preset's 1.5 m limit, so distant room surfaces stay out; room scans keep their existing range.
- **Live Room shares its furniture even when it is still points.** A shell without a usable furniture surface includes its fallback furniture as coloured points in the exported GLB, with the same placement as on screen.
- **Live Room keeps photographed planes for scans without dense geometry.** Captures without raw depth, or without usable depth points, retain the textured replay they open on.
- **Live Room measures the whole floor.** The ground covers the shell's floor faces, including rectangular interiors, while leaving L-shaped and T-shaped cutouts outside.
- **3D Scan uses less working memory when rebuilding a result.** Depth fusion releases its TSDF before hole closing and simplification, reuses owned mesh arrays, and ground loading reads only positions and normals.
- **3D Scan results open closer.** Rooms and objects fill the shorter screen side and make one gentle quarter-turn on opening. A touch stops the move at once, reduced motion skips it, and Reset returns to the same close view.
