- **Point & Ask no longer dead-ends silently on a phone that supports Gemini Nano ([#2648](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/2648)).** A model AICore was already downloading is now followed with a percent and a progress bar instead of an endless spinner; a failed download offers "Retry download"; "unavailable" names its reason (no AICore, AICore still setting up, AICore too old, check failed or timed out) and always offers "Check again", an AICore update where it can help, and the on-device AR Object Labels demo as a fallback, with no cloud model. The availability check and the first answer token are time-boxed, AICore setup codes 601/606 are retryable, and every visible state is logged under `adb logcat -s PointAndAsk`.
