- **iOS demo: Cosmos moves like Android.** The ribbon dashes of the star, burst and flow scenes now travel, stars and sparks twinkle, and the star's plasma surface churns instead of only turning — all without a custom shader. The churn bakes half-size frames in the background and pauses in Low Power Mode or when the device runs hot. The Flow scene turns a quarter in landscape like Android, and the star gets more cell contrast and a wider blue limb halo. On macOS, demos now open in a sheet large enough to use instead of a thin strip.
