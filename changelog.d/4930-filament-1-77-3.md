<!-- category: Changed -->
- **Android now runs on Filament 1.77.3 ([#4930](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4930)).** Every Android material blob is recompiled for it (material version 77). `Scene.removeEntity`, `Engine.isValid()` and the SurfaceTexture `Stream.Builder.stream` were removed upstream: SceneView now uses `Scene.remove`, a new `Engine.isValid` extension and `StreamHelper.setStreamSource`. The web runtimes are unchanged.
