- **android-demo**: Room Scan has a Plan view for your scans, next to 3D, Map and Camera. It gives the room's survey — floor area, length × width, perimeter, ceiling height and the length of each wall — and a dimensioned floor plan drawn to scale, and shares both as a one-page A4 PDF or as a few lines of text. The walls are measured where the scan saw them, not on the extent of what it saw: a wall seen on less than half its length is dashed and its length marked "≈", every figure carries the spread it was read with, and when fewer than two pairs of facing walls were seen the view gives no area and says what to scan ("Scan the missing wall", "Raise the phone to the ceiling"). Indicative plan, not a certified measurement — the screen, the PDF and the text all say so. A scan's "Export 3D files" moves from the dock to the settings sheet (#4540).
