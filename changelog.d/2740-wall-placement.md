- `WallPlacementScene` — a one-call AR flow for placing products on a **vertical surface** (TV, framed art, mirror, shelf), the vertical-surface sibling of `PlacementScene`. Inspired by Amazon "AR View" / IKEA Place: orientation is taken from the wall (object flush + upright, no hit-pose tilt) and height from the floor (`floorY + mountHeight`), so a placement stays put while the vertical plane jitters. Tracks the live **floor↔wall seam** (`onSeamChanged`) and the onboarding phase (`onPhaseChanged`). The placement geometry is exposed as pure, unit-tested functions — `wallFacingRotation`, `floorWallSeam`, `wallAnchorPose` — for custom flows. First increment of #2740; an in-scene 3D seam guide line and a gizmo/D-pad fine-adjust UI are tracked follow-ups.
