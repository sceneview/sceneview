<!-- category: Fixed -->
- **A `ModelNode` no longer crashes once its model is destroyed ([#4827](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4827)).** gltfio frees a model's instance and animator without clearing their handles, and the node can outlive its model by a few frames when `rememberModelInstance` leaves the composition first. The node's frame, `stopAnimation` (called on dispose), a second `safeDestroyModel`, the scene inspector, the editing overlay, `rememberModelAnimationState` (reads idle) and `releaseSourceData` after a synchronous load now skip the destroyed model instead of reading freed memory. The `ModelNode` composable — in `SceneView`, `AutoPlacementScene` and `SceneViewer` — shows nothing for a destroyed model instead of throwing from its own recomposition. `boundingBox` and the values derived from it, `animationCount`, `playAnimation`, `setAnimationSpeed`, the `ModelNode` constructor and the `ModelInstance.materialInstances` extension throw an `IllegalStateException` that names the cause, and `getMaterialInstanceAt` throws for an entity without a renderable or a primitive out of range instead of crashing in native code.

<!-- category: Added -->
- **`Model.isDestroyed`, `ModelInstance.isDestroyed` and `ModelNode.isModelDestroyed` ([#4827](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4827)).** Check them before reading the bounds or starting an animation of a model whose lifetime is not yours: those calls throw once the model is destroyed.
