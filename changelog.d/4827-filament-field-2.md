<!-- category: Fixed -->
- **A `ModelNode` no longer crashes once its model is destroyed ([#4827](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4827)).** gltfio frees a model's instance and animator without clearing their handles, and the node can outlive its model by a few frames when `rememberModelInstance` leaves the composition first. The node's frame, `stopAnimation` (called on dispose), a second `safeDestroyModel`, the scene inspector, the editing overlay, `rememberModelAnimationState` (reads idle) and `releaseSourceData` after a synchronous load now skip the destroyed model instead of reading freed memory. The `ModelNode` composable — in `SceneView`, `AutoPlacementScene` and `SceneViewer` — shows nothing for a destroyed model instead of throwing from its own recomposition. `boundingBox` and the values derived from it (`scaleToUnitCube` and `centerOrigin` included), `animationCount`, `playAnimation`, `setAnimationSpeed`, `skinCount`, `skinNames`, `materialVariantNames`, `attachSkin`, `detachSkin`, `getJointCount`, the sub-nodes' `name`, `extras`, `morphTargetNames` and `getMaterialInstanceAt`, the `ModelNode` constructor and the `ModelInstance.materialInstances` extension throw an `IllegalStateException` that names the cause, and `getMaterialInstanceAt` throws for an entity without a renderable or a primitive out of range instead of crashing in native code. The renderable state set or read by entity (`setMaterialInstance`, `isShadowCaster`, `setPriority`…) stays unguarded: Filament ignores a destroyed entity, and the `isModelDestroyed` KDoc lists each member on both sides.

<!-- category: Added -->
- **`Model.isDestroyed`, `ModelInstance.isDestroyed` and `ModelNode.isModelDestroyed` ([#4827](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4827)).** Check them before reading the bounds or starting an animation of a model whose lifetime is not yours: those calls throw once the model is destroyed.

<!-- category: Changed -->
- **A resource resolver that destroys the model it is resolving now makes the load throw ([#4827](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4827)).** A resolver passed to `ModelLoader.createModel`, `createInstancedModel` or `createModelInstance` that calls `clear()`, `destroy()` or `destroyModel` on its loader used to leave gltfio reading the freed asset. The loader now stops at the first resolver call after the destroy, asks gltfio nothing more and throws an `IllegalStateException` that names the cause. The suspend loaders already returned `null` in that case and keep doing so.
