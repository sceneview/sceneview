- **A `ModelNode` no longer crashes once its model is destroyed ([#4827](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4827)).** gltfio frees a model's instance and animator without clearing their handles, and the node can outlive its model by a few frames when `rememberModelInstance` leaves the composition first. The node's frame, `stopAnimation` (called on dispose), a second `safeDestroyModel`, the scene inspector and the editing overlay now skip the destroyed model instead of reading freed memory. `boundingBox` and the values derived from it, `animationCount`, `playAnimation`, `setAnimationSpeed` and the `ModelNode` constructor throw an `IllegalStateException` that names the cause, and `getMaterialInstanceAt` throws for an entity without a renderable or a primitive out of range instead of crashing in native code.
