- **Demo: "Museum & Space" in the Model Viewer (Android).** Four real-world scans join the HD pack: the Apollo 11 Command Module and its cut-away interior (Smithsonian, CC0), a woolly mammoth skeleton (Smithsonian, CC0) and the Perseverance rover (NASA/JPL-Caltech, insignia removed). Each opens at its real size, its thumbnail stands on the stage until the file is on the device, and the pill now tracks that model's own file — "queued" while another one downloads, the model on screen fetched first. Perseverance holds its pose instead of playing its 23 animations at once. Credits reach About → Credits from the manifest.
