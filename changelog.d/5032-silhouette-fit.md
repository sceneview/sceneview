- Auto-fit now frames fully skinned, animated content with silhouette probes over separate sampled clip poses instead of only its rest-pose box. Concurrent playback falls back to the measured composed pose, and fitting follows automatic centering until it settles; static, geometric, and mixed content keeps corner fitting. Animation envelopes remain cached per asset and clip with bounded sampling and rest-pose fallback. ([#5032](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5032))
