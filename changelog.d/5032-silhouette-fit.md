- **Auto-fit frames a skinned, animated model on its clips' poses, not on its rest-pose box ([#5032](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5032)).** `SceneView(autoFitContent = true)` now takes `fitMode: FitMode? = null`: when every model in the content is skinned and animated and nothing else renders, it fits `FitMode.Silhouette` probes over the envelope of the playing clips (all clips when none plays, at most 8, 24 poses each, every pose kept separate). Static models, geometry and mixed scenes keep `FitMode.Corners`, so a box-shaped product is never cropped; pass `fitMode = FitMode.Corners` to keep the old framing everywhere. `SceneAutoFitState.maybeFit` follows the same rule, raw-box helpers (`fitCameraToBounds`, `fitToBounds`, `frameToBounds`) are unchanged, and the JVM signatures are the same. New `Model.animationEnvelope(clips: Collection<Int>, samples)` samples several clips at once. The demo's Model Viewer frames rigged, animated models the same way.
