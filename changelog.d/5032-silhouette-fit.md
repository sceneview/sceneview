- Auto-fit now frames fully skinned, animated content with silhouette probes over sampled clip poses instead of only its rest-pose box. Sampling includes rigid renderables, composes retained or concurrent clip channels, preserves the last pose when playback is paused, and follows automatic centering until it settles; static, geometric, incompletely sampled, and mixed content keeps corner fitting with measured bounds. Animation envelopes remain cached per asset and clip with bounded sampling and rest-pose fallback. ([#5032](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5032))
