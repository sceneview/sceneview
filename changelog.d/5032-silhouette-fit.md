- Auto-fit now frames fully skinned, animated content with silhouette probes over separate sampled clip poses instead of only its rest-pose box. Static, geometric, and mixed content keeps corner fitting, while explicit fit modes remain available. Animation envelopes are cached per asset and clip with bounded sampling that prioritizes the last-applied clips, plus rest-pose fallback. ([#5032](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5032))
