- **Demo app: the Body Tracker no longer freezes the camera feed, and it is now a Squat Coach ([#4604](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4604)).** Pose detection used to run on the thread that draws the camera, through a JPEG encode and decode and a blocking MediaPipe call, several times a second. The camera image is now converted straight from YUV on a background thread, MediaPipe runs in live-stream mode and answers asynchronously, and a frame is skipped rather than queued while a detection is still running. The screen counts squats from the knee angle, says "Lower" when a rep is not deep enough, and shows the count large enough to read from across the room.
