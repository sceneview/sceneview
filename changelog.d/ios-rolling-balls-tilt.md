- **iOS demo: Rolling Balls tilts by default, further and smoothly.** Tilt is now on when the demo opens, so dragging the scene tips the tray and the balls roll; turning Tilt off gives the drag back to the camera orbit. The tray tips up to 35° (was 20°), by drag or with the sliders, and the camera frames the whole tray at that angle. The drag and the sliders set a target angle that the tray eases onto on every display frame, and the tray and the balls' gravity are updated together in that frame, which removes the small jolts in the tray's motion. After an orbit, a drag to the right still lowers the side of the tray that is on the right of the screen. Balls now roll like solid spheres with a gentle rolling resistance: they speed up realistically on a slope, stay put on a nearly level tray, and come to a stop on a flat one.
