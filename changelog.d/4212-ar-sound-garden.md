- **Demo apps: Sound Garden, a song you walk through in AR ([#4212](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4212)).** Four glowing orbs planted on the floor each play one part of the same loop, on Android and iOS. With headphones, the part you walk up to gets louder and the ones behind you sound muffled. Each orb pulses with its own part and sends a wave of light out on every note, so you can see where each sound comes from. Tap an orb to mute its part.
