- **arsceneview**: `ArDepthFrame` (metric depth in millimetres, with confidence, intrinsics, camera pose and a `source` of `ARCORE` or `ML`) and `MlDepthSession`. The session turns any `MonocularDepthEstimator` into metric depth frames: it runs the network off the render thread at up to 5 Hz, scales its relative inverse depth to metres with a robust fit (`1/z = s·d + t`) on ARCore plane samples and feature points, smooths the scale over time, and pauses when the phone gets hot. It publishes on a `StateFlow`. Feed it from `onSessionUpdated`.
- **arsceneview-depth-ml** (new module, not published yet): `DepthAnythingV2Estimator` runs Depth Anything V2 **Small** (Apache-2.0) on LiteRT, on CPU or GPU. The 27.7 MB model is downloaded on first use from a pinned revision and checked against its SHA-256, so the APK carries none of it.
- **Android demo**: the Depth Visualization demo gains an **ARCore | ML** pill. ML mode shows the estimate in metres on the same false-colour ramp, with a status card (download, waiting for surfaces, ms per estimate, Hz, anchors, fit error). It also works on devices without ARCore's Depth API.
