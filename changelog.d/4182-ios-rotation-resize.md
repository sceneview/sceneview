- **SceneViewSwift — rotation in the iOS Simulator**: a `SceneView` now fills the screen after the Simulator rotates. In the iOS 26 Simulator, RealityKit kept the `RealityView` render surface at its launch size, so after portrait → landscape the scene only covered a portrait-wide strip and the rest of the screen was black. In the Simulator, `SceneView` now resizes the surface to the view and refreshes the camera projection. Real devices are unchanged: an iPhone already resizes the surface on its own, and the workaround stretched the scene there, so it is compiled for the Simulator only (#4182).
