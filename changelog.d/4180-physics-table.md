- **Rolling Balls now plays on a proper games table, and you can pick the balls up ([#4180](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4180)).** The tray is now a green felt bed inside a lacquered walnut rim with a brass inlay. The balls and the rim cast soft shadows on the felt, and the camera frames the whole table. Touch a ball and drag it across the felt, then let go mid-swipe to throw it: it leaves at the speed of your finger and knocks the other balls aside. Tilt is now on when the demo opens. Dragging the empty felt tips the table, and with Tilt off it turns the camera around the table instead.
