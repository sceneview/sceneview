- **Rolling Balls now plays on a proper games table, and you can pick the balls up ([#4180](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4180)).** The tray is now a green felt bed inside a lacquered walnut rim with a brass inlay. The balls and the rim cast soft shadows on the felt, and the table fills the width of the screen, centred between the title and the controls. Touch a ball and drag it across the felt, then let go mid-swipe to throw it: it leaves at the speed of your finger and knocks the other balls aside. A second finger on the screen doesn't take the ball away, and lifting the finger that holds it always lets it go. Tilt is now on when the demo opens. Dragging the empty felt tips the table, and with Tilt off it turns the camera around the table instead.
