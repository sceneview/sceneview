- **Android AR resume failures (#4756).** A failed resume publishes the existing retryable session failure state; successful resume clears it. Retrying preserves a live session and its anchors, creating a session only when absent or closed. Retrying from a paused or detached host does nothing. Display-geometry and host-callback exceptions propagate during direct resume; during a posted camera handover they are logged and reported through `onArSessionFailed`.
