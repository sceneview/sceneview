- **Android demo: no preview card shows the Damaged Helmet any more ([#4951](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4951)).** The Model Viewer, Lighting, Camera & object controls and Performance test cards were AI-generated pictures of the Khronos Damaged Helmet, whose CC-BY-NC sculpt may not appear in a store app. They are now crops of what each demo renders on the emulator, in both themes: the CC0 Flight Helmet, the CC-BY motorcycle helmet with its light probes, the whole camera stage, and the stress test's 1,000 spheres. The two AR occlusion cards cannot be captured on an emulator, so they are generated again, from a capture of the motorcycle helmet that those demos place: behind a chair leg, and behind a passer-by. The generator's helmet reference and every prompt that used it move to the same CC-BY subject.
