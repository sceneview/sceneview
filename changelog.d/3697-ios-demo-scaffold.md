- **iOS demo: one `DemoScaffold` for every demo screen ([#3697](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/3697)).** The scene, glass back button, title, accessory slot, floating dock and settings sheet now come from a single SwiftUI scaffold that handles the bottom safe area once; the bottom-of-screen dimensions are written in pt in `DESIGN.md`. Glass chrome gains a ceiling and a 24 % border so it keeps a visible edge over bright media (container vs ground 1.01:1 → ≥ 1.43:1), and the settings sheet rests on the measured height of its controls instead of cutting through them. `TextDemo` and `BillboardDemo` are rewritten on it.
