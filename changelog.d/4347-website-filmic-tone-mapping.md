- **The website's model viewers tone-map with Filmic, like Android and the Kotlin/JS bundle ([#4347](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4347)).** The hand-written `website-static/js/sceneview.js` (site pages and `/web-demo/`) left its view on Filament's default ACES (legacy) curve. It now sets a Filmic `ColorGrading`, destroyed with the viewer in `dispose()`. Saturated colours keep more of their saturation. On an opaque canvas the background colour goes through the tone mapper too, so it shifts slightly (the default dark slate `#333c57` renders as `#3c4564`); a transparent canvas still shows the page untouched.
