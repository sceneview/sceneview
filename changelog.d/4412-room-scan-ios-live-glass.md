- **iOS demo: Room Scan rebuilds the room on glass while it scans ([#4412](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4412)).** While a scan records, the camera is the screen: one pill carries the recording dot, the clock and the point count, and next to it a live 3D card shows the room being rebuilt, on glass, so the camera stays visible through it — and through every control around it, which share that glass. A tap grows the card to the whole row, a drag turns the room, its corner button shrinks it back. On its side the card keeps the leading edge.
- **iOS demo: Room Scan says when a scan is full ([#4412](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4412)).** A scan holds 12,000 points, 300 photos and five minutes, and used to stop at each silently. From 80 % of a budget the screen counts against it ("10k / 12k"), then reads "12k · full" with one notice: "Point limit reached — new areas add no more points." The five minutes are announced from 4:00.
- **iOS demo: Room Scan counts the surface found, in m² ([#4412](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4412)).** The settings sheet reads the points and the photos against their budgets, the surface found as an area instead of a count of patches that fell whenever two merged, and the scan's kind.
