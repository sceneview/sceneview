- **`ViewNode`: a drag that starts on an interactive card now orbits the camera ([#4033](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4033)).** Any Compose container consumes the `DOWN` it is hit by, so a forwarded card used to keep the whole gesture and the camera never moved when a drag began on it. Once the pointer passes the touch slop, or a second finger comes down, and nothing inside the card has claimed the gesture (a scrolling list or a slider still does, through `requestDisallowInterceptTouchEvent`), the card gets an `ACTION_CANCEL` and the scene takes the stream over, with its `DOWN` replayed so the orbit starts without a jump. Taps still click. New `Node.takeTouchStreamHandBack()` hook; `SceneView` and `ARSceneView` both honour it.
- **`ViewNode` (lit): the corners around a rounded card are transparent again ([#4032](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4032)).** `view_texture_lit.mat` used `blending: transparent`, which applies alpha to diffuse light only, so the transparent texels around a rounded Compose card still reflected the environment and showed as a dark rectangle. It now uses `blending: fade`; opaque pixels are unchanged. The blob is recompiled with the pinned `matc` 1.72.1.
- **Demo app, Picking & Collision: a picked shape is unmistakably lit ([#4031](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4031)).** The highlight colour was also one of the shapes' default colours, so lighting that shape (and its near-twin) changed almost nothing on screen. A picked shape now turns amber (the existing `warning` accent token) and grows slightly.
