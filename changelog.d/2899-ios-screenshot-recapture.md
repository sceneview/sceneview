- **The iOS App Store screenshots are re-captured from a post-#2897 build**, clearing the ⛔ caveat that blocked reconciling the App Store listing drift ([#2899](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/2899)). The four frames were shot while `SceneEnvironment.intensity` was still applied as a `2^x` exponent; they now come from a build with the linear multiplier. The visible change is nil, exactly as the caveat's own measurement predicted — which is the point: the frames are now provably what the app renders, instead of being *probably close enough*.
- **Looking at the re-captured mosaic surfaced two problems a green capture cannot see.** `02-dynamic-sky.png` renders iOS's stylised five-cube skyline (`DynamicSkyDemo`) while Android publishes a photoreal lit drone for the same demo id — so shipping the iOS set as-is would contradict the [#2773](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/2773) "same demos, framed the same way" intent. And the iPad frames leak their capture date (`09:41 Tue 28 Jul` vs `09:41 Mon 3 Aug`), because `simctl status_bar override --time` does not cover the date iPadOS draws beside the clock — which both dates a public listing and defeats the script's byte-reproducibility. Both are now recorded in `samples/ios-demo/appstore-screenshots/README.md`; the upload stays blocked pending a decision on the iOS set.
