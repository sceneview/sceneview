- **A closed ARCore session is never called again ([#4026](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4026)).** ARCore's `Session.close()` frees the native session but keeps the Java handle, so any later call on it crashed the app outright, past every `try`/`catch`. `ARSession` now closes once and exposes `isClosed`. `pause()`, `updateOrNull()` and `setDisplayGeometry()` do nothing on a closed session, and `resume()` throws. `rememberARPlaybackStatus` stops polling and returns `NONE`, and `ARRecorder` skips its status poll and `stopRecording()`. The recorder case happened when a screen was left mid-recording: the `ARSceneView` closed its session before the outer `rememberARRecorder` stopped. `AnchorNode.destroy()` now runs once, so it can't detach its anchor after its session is gone.
