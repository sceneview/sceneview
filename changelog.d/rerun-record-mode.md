- **The Rerun demo can now scan your own room in 3D.** "Scan your own room" in the replay's dock opens the camera; tap record and walk the phone slowly around the room. While it records, the screen counts the points, surfaces and photos in large figures, and a 3D card under them draws the scan as it grows: the camera's path, the feature points in the colours the camera saw, the planes, and a photo in its frustum every 15 cm or 10°. Tap stop and the scan opens in the same 3D replay as the bundled room, with its filmstrip, orbit and scrubbing. Nothing is simulated and nothing leaves the phone: the scan lives in memory until you leave the demo.
