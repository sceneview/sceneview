- **Every circulating count of demos and node types was re-derived from source, and sixteen sites corrected ([#3192](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3192)).** The Android demo count appeared as 43, 47, 52 and 53 across eight files; the ground truth is **54**, and the split is **19 non-AR + 35 AR** — read from `DemoEntry.category` in the 55 `*Fragment.kt` files (one of which is the `DemoFragment` base interface), which is also what `GeneratedDemos.fragments` lists. The published "18 non-AR + 34 AR" and "19 non-AR + 34 AR" splits were both wrong on the AR half. The iOS page claimed **59 demos** against a registry holding 69 deep-link ids of which 21 are coming-soon cards, so **48** are actually shipped; it now states all three numbers rather than a fourth that matched none of them.
- **The Swift node-type count circulated as 16, 19 and 20 because each had a defensible definition and none was written down ([#3192](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3192)).** `Nodes/` holds 20 files; one is the `ShapePresets` enum, and of the 19 remaining `*Node` types, `SceneReconstructionNode` is a static-only namespace rather than an instantiable node. The count is now **19** everywhere — the same rule that already derives Android's 27 (31 files under `node/`, four of them the `Node` base and its delegates/state) — and every site states its derivation, so the next reader can check it instead of copying it. `mcp/src/platform-setup.ts` returns that table verbatim to an assistant and had been missing `ShapeNode`, `ViewNode` and `SceneReconstructionNode` for three releases; they are added, along with a note that `AnchorNode` and `SpatialAudioNode` live outside the folder and are deliberately not counted.
- **`website-static/js/sceneview.js` self-declared version 4.18.0 while `VERSION_NAME` was 4.30.0** — in a JSDoc banner and in `SceneView.version`, the value any page reads at runtime. Twelve minor versions of drift on a shipped file that `sync-versions.sh`'s 118 checks do not cover.
- **`docs/docs/structured-data.json` advertised `list_node_types`**, a tool removed from every registry, in the schema.org FAQ answer that search engines and assistants quote when asked what the MCP server does. Replaced with `list_samples`, which exists. See the companion entry on why the gate could not see this file.
- **The Maestro master flow claimed to drive "every one of the 51 registered demos"; it drives 68 legs covering 47 of 54.** Its per-category table was wrong in two rows (Lighting 4→5, AR 33→35) and its total by three. Seven registered demos have **no QA leg at all** — `animation-physics`, `ar-measure`, `camera-gestures`, `custom-geometry`, `lighting-lab`, `picking-collision`, `two-d-in-three-d`. The header no longer overstates the coverage and names the seven; closing the gap needs a parity gate rather than a header edit, and nothing today reconciles the two lists. All 20 flow ids with no registered demo were verified as declared aliases in `DeepLinkRouter.DEMO_ID_ALIASES`.
