- **Demo app: Finger Drawing, a new demo to paint in 3D with one finger ([#4082](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4082)).** Each stroke is a smooth, round tube of glossy paint, thicker where your finger slows down. Turn the stage with Orbit and the next stroke lies on a new plane, so a few strokes from a few angles make a small sculpture. Pick one of five colours and a thickness, undo the last stroke or clear the stage. An AR mode paints the same strokes an arm's reach in front of the phone and hangs them on ARCore anchors, so they stay in the room while you walk around them. When the phone loses its place, after a trip to the home screen for instance, the app says how to bring the drawing back, and a new stroke is always painted where you can see it. The colour and thickness you picked follow you between the 3D and AR modes.
