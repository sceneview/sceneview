- **Name and measure a tapped object in a scan's replay ([#4485](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4485)).** In the Android demo, a tap on a piece of furniture in the replay of a 3D scan shows its oriented box with its length, depth and height, in feet and inches in US English and metres elsewhere. When scene recognition ([#4763](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4763)) recognised that object, the box carries its name. An object the scan saw from one side only is marked as possibly measuring short, because its box is missing the side the scan never saw. The box is computed off the main thread.
