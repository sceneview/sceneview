- **The Rerun demo now records your own room in 3D, and keeps your sessions on the phone in the same format as the iOS demo ([#4063](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4063)).** The demo opens on a landing laid out like the iOS demo's: "Scan a room in 3D", a primary "Record your room" ("Everything stays on your phone."), "Watch a sample session", "Open file", and "Your sessions". Record opens the camera and starts as soon as ARCore has found the room. Walk the phone slowly around. While it records, the screen shows the points, surfaces and photos in large figures. A 3D card under them draws the scan as it grows: the camera's path, the feature points in the colours the camera saw, the planes, and a photo in its frustum every 15 cm or 10°. Tap stop and the scan is saved on the phone. It then opens in the same 3D replay as the sample, with the same filmstrip, orbit and scrubbing, and its floor and walls are painted from its own photos. Saved sessions stay under "Your sessions" across launches, each with its first photo, its date and source, and its path, points, photos and length. Each card's menu shares the session as a `.svscan` scan file, or deletes it. A `.svscan` is a plain uncompressed zip of the recorder's three files, byte for byte the file the iOS demo writes, so a session moves between the two apps. "Open file", and "Open with" from another app, read a `.svscan` back into "Your sessions" and replay it. A file this version cannot read gets a message that says why; Rerun `.rrd` files are not read yet. Nothing is simulated and nothing leaves the phone unless you share it. Streaming a live session to Rerun on a computer is still there, as an advanced option in the settings sheet.
