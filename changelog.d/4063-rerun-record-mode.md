- **The Rerun demo now records your own room in 3D, and keeps your scans on the phone ([#4063](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4063)).** The demo opens on a landing with the sample room turning in 3D behind it. It has three things on it: "Record your room", "Watch a sample session", and "Your sessions". Record opens the camera and starts as soon as ARCore has found the room. Walk the phone slowly around. While it records, the screen shows the points, surfaces and photos in large figures. A 3D card under them draws the scan as it grows: the camera's path, the feature points in the colours the camera saw, the planes, and a photo in its frustum every 15 cm or 10°. Tap stop and the scan is saved on the phone. It then opens in the same 3D replay as the sample, with the same filmstrip, orbit and scrubbing, and its floor and walls are painted from its own photos. Saved scans stay under "Your sessions" across launches, each with a cover, its length and its counts, until you delete them. Nothing is simulated and nothing leaves the phone. Streaming a live session to Rerun on a computer is still there, as an advanced option in the settings sheet.
