- **Android demo: the cards changed in 4.52.0 say so ([#4320](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4320)).** Thirteen demos that gained a mode, a reworked screen or a visible fix in 4.52.0 (Cosmos, Rolling Balls, Camera & Gestures, AR Placement, Cloud Anchors, Geospatial Anchors, Depth Visualization, AR Measure, Plane Node, Plane Renderer V2, Scene Geometry, Splat Room and Rerun) now declare `updatedIn = "4.52.0"`. Eight of them declared 4.51.0 and would have lost their "Updated" chip one release early, Splat Room declared 4.49.0 and Depth Visualization, AR Measure, Plane Node and Scene Geometry declared no update, so those five carried no chip at all. A "Coming soon" card is never marked, as on iOS: the headset-only Android XR card no longer reads "Updated".
