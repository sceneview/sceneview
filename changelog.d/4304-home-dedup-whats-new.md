- **Demo app: the top of the Home shows each sample once, and "What's new" no longer hides the catalogue ([#4304](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4304)).** On Android the same sample could lead the header pager, the "What's new" row and the "Featured" banners one after the other. A sample is now pushed once above the catalogue (pager, then Featured, then What's new): the pager keeps Models and Cosmos, the Featured banners keep AR placement, AR Rerun and Materials, and the full list does not repeat a banner under it. Tapping the "What's new" row opens the "What's new" sheet over the full catalogue instead of selecting the filter, which made the app look like it only had those samples. The filter chips stay for who wants the catalogue cut down, and a filter now always shows its way out: tapping the selected chip again goes back to "All", Back clears the filter before it leaves the app, and a filtered list ends on a "Show all N samples" button.
