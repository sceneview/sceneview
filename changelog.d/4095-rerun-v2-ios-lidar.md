- **Demo (iOS):** Rerun Record captures a dense coloured point cloud on iPhones and iPads with LiDAR, the iOS twin of the Android depth capture: ARKit scene depth is sampled ten times a second where it is at least medium-confidence, back-projected and fused into 2 cm surfels with normals, saved in the `.svscan` file as `dense/points.bin` (format v2, byte-identical to Android's for the same cloud), revealed by the replay as it grew, and exported to `.ply` (with normals), `.glb` and `.rrd` (`world/dense`, which comes back as the dense cloud when the `.rrd` is reopened in the app). A `.svscan` v2 recorded on either platform opens on the other; devices without LiDAR record exactly as before.
