- **iOS demo — Museum & Space:** the Model Viewer's picker gets a "Museum & Space" section with four HD pack models, at parity with Android: the Smithsonian **Apollo 11 Command Module** and **Apollo 11 Interior** (CC0), the Smithsonian **Woolly Mammoth** skeleton (CC0) and NASA's **Perseverance Rover** (NASA media guidelines, insignia removed). None ships in the App Store build. Before the download the stage shows the model's thumbnail with the HD pill ("Apollo 11 Interior · download 38 MB"), then the model at its real-world size, read from a new optional `scale` field of the shared `assets/hd-pack/ios.json` manifest. Perseverance opens still: its 23 mechanism clips stay under Animate, paused. These models are not offered in AR yet. Their credits appear in the app's Credits screen, read from the manifest.
