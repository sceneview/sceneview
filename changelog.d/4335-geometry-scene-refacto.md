- **Android demo: the Geometry scene shows the seven primitives and is usable in landscape ([#4335](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4335)).** Geometry Primitives drew four of the seven built-in shapes on a black background, re-centred the others each time one was hidden, and in landscape put its subject under the chips row. It now gives `CubeNode`, `SphereNode`, `CylinderNode`, `ConeNode`, `TorusNode`, `CapsuleNode` and `PlaneNode` a fixed slot each in a studio environment, fitted to the band between the title and the chips with `contentPadding` and `fitCameraToBounds`: a 2-3-2 block in portrait, a single row on a landscape phone, through a lens that lengthens with the band so the row is not stretched. Hiding a shape moves nothing, the spin can be paused, Recenter and Reset bring the camera home (also while paused), and Metallic 1 with Roughness 0 gives mirrors instead of black faces. The Showcase card is a capture of the scene.
