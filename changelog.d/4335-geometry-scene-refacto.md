- **Android demo: the Geometry scene shows the seven primitives and is usable in landscape ([#4335](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4335)).** Geometry Primitives drew four of the seven built-in shapes on a black background, re-centred the others each time one was hidden, and in landscape put its subject under the chips row. It now gives `CubeNode`, `SphereNode`, `CylinderNode`, `ConeNode`, `TorusNode`, `CapsuleNode` and `PlaneNode` a fixed slot each on the stage, in tones that read against it and under a studio light, fitted to the band between the title and the chips with `contentPadding` and `fitCameraToBounds`: a 2-3-2 block in portrait, a single row on a landscape phone, through a lens that lengthens with the band so the row is not stretched. Hiding a shape moves nothing, the spin can be paused, Recenter and Reset bring the camera home (also while paused), and at Metallic 1 with Roughness 0 the shapes mirror a studio instead of an empty scene (the round ones catch its lights; a flat face still goes dark while it faces a dark part of it). The shape chips are a legend — a dot of the shape's colour, a hollow ring and a struck-through name once hidden — drawn by a glass chip Rolling Balls now shares. The Showcase card is a capture of the scene.
