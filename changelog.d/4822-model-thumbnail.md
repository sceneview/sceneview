- **Model thumbnails rendered off screen: `ModelSnapshot` and `rememberModelThumbnail`.** A GLB becomes a `Bitmap` with no visible view: framed whole on its bounding box, seen in three-quarter view from slightly above (`ModelSnapshotOptions` changes the angle, lens and margin), lit like a default `SceneView`, on a transparent background. Requests queue and render one at a time on the screen's engine, suspending between frames so a grid of twenty never blocks one; the renderer and lights are reused, and results are kept in an optional LRU cache. The Android demo gains a **Model Thumbnails** screen: the bundled models, twice each, with the time they took.
