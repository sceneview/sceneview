- **Model thumbnails rendered off screen: `ModelSnapshot` and `rememberModelThumbnail`.** A GLB becomes a `Bitmap` with no visible view: framed whole on its bounding box, seen in three-quarter view from slightly above (`ModelSnapshotOptions` changes the angle, lens and margin), lit like a default `SceneView`, on a transparent background. Requests queue and render one model at a time on the screen's engine (each costs the main thread what loading that model into a scene costs); the renderer and lights are reused, and complete results are kept in an optional LRU cache. `rememberModelThumbnail` returns a `ModelThumbnailState` — `Loading`, `Ready(bitmap)` or `Failed(reason)` — so an unloadable, empty, quantized (`KHR_mesh_quantization`) or fully transparent model is reported instead of leaving its cell empty. The API is `@ExperimentalSceneViewApi`. The Android demo gains a **Model Thumbnails** screen: the bundled models, twice each, with the time they took.
