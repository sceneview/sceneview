- **Demo app: "Your Room, as a Dollhouse" no longer freezes, says which recording stands, and Reset really resets ([#4075](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4075)).** Going from AR to the 3D view and back reused a placement state the first AR view had dismissed, so the room never stood again; each AR view now takes a fresh one. Reset lifts the room and waits a moment before standing it where you aim, back at 1:12, instead of putting it straight back. The recording on the table is named, with its date, and a tap lists every kept recording to stand another. A recording that kept no floor, wall or table says so and offers to record again, instead of standing an empty plinth. The plinth now has a thickness and a contact shadow, and the surfaces are drawn in solid colour where there is no photo. The Real size button keeps its name and lights up while the room stands at real size, so it always agrees with the scale shown above the dock (it read "Miniature", lit, over "Real size"). At real size the room no longer stands centred half a metre from the camera, its walls and floor cutting through the view: its near side sits where the miniature stood and it reaches away from you, turned so the side you started recording from faces you.
