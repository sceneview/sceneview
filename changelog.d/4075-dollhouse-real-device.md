- **Demo app: "Your Room, as a Dollhouse" no longer freezes, says which recording stands, and Reset really resets ([#4075](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4075)).** Going from AR to the 3D view and back reused a placement state the first AR view had dismissed, so the room never stood again; each AR view now takes a fresh one. Reset lifts the room and waits a moment before standing it where you aim, back at 1:12, instead of putting it straight back. The recording on the table is named, with its date, and a tap lists every kept recording to stand another. A recording that kept no floor, wall or table says so and offers to record again, instead of standing an empty plinth. The plinth now has a thickness and a contact shadow, and the surfaces are drawn in solid colour where there is no photo.
