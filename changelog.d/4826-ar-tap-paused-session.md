- **A tap on a paused AR session no longer crashes the app ([#4826](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4826)).** `ARSceneView` confirms a single tap from a `Handler` after the double-tap timeout, so an `onSingleTapConfirmed` that called `HitResult.createAnchor()` could run after the ARCore session paused and throw `SessionPausedException` or `FatalException` from the main looper, out of the app's reach. The gesture listener passed to `ARSceneView` is now guarded: those ARCore session errors, and `NotTrackingException`, are logged and the gesture is dropped, while any other exception still surfaces. Node drags that hit-test the last frame (`PoseNode`, auto-placement) are guarded the same way.
