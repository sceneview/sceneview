- **iOS demo: Room Scan gives the screen back to the scene ([#4379](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4379)).** The replay (formerly "Rerun AR Replay") stood a status card, a live-camera picture-in-picture and a tall timeline card on the scanned room, which kept about a quarter of an iPhone held upright and nothing clear on one held sideways. It now keeps one glass timeline bar (play, filmstrip scrubber, clock) above the dock, or in the title row in landscape, and a tap on the room hides every control, the status bar included. What is read once moved into the settings sheet: the Path, Planes, Points and Anchors layers with their figures at the playhead, and the room's size measured from the recorded planes. The room is framed in the band the chrome leaves, so opening the sheet lifts it instead of covering it. A scan in progress says one line (Scanning, the clock) in the title row.
