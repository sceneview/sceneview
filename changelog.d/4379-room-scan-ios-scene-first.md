- **iOS demo: Room Scan gives the screen back to the scene ([#4379](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4379)).** The replay (formerly "Rerun AR Replay") stood a status card, a live-camera picture-in-picture and a tall timeline card on the scanned room, which kept about a quarter of an iPhone held upright and nothing clear on one held sideways. It now keeps one glass timeline bar (play, filmstrip scrubber, clock) above the dock, or in the title row in landscape, and a tap on the room hides every control, the status bar included. What is read once moved into the settings sheet: the Path, Planes, Points and Anchors layers with their figures at the playhead, and the room's size measured from the recorded planes. The whole room — planes, walls and path — is fitted in the band the chrome leaves with a margin on every side, upright or sideways, so no wall is cut at the screen's edge, opening the sheet lifts the room instead of covering it, and hiding the controls frames it in the whole screen. A scan in progress says one line (Scanning, the clock) in the title row.
