- **Android demo: no third-party trademark on the demo's own scenes and cards ([#5247](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5247)).** The bundled motorcycle helmet carried a legible Harley-Davidson badge: its badge textures are now plain leather (a modified CC-BY derivative, noted in the credits), so the Lighting, Camera & object controls, Secondary Camera, AR Image and AR occlusion scenes and the Model Viewer's stand-in show no mark. The Camera & object controls stage swaps the Nike Air Jordan 1 and the Canon AT-1 for the Khronos Toy Car and Lantern (CC0); Camera paths orbits the Toy Car instead of the Peugeot 2008; Portrait focus (AR depth of field) focuses on the Toy Car instead of the sneaker; AR Image stands the helmet on the print instead of the SMEG toaster; Point & Ask drops a dining chair, an armchair or a floor lamp; and the AR placement picker no longer lists the SMEG toaster and the Air Jordan 1. The Lighting, Camera & object controls and both AR occlusion cards are regenerated. The Model Viewer's product catalogue keeps every branded product the user can pick.
