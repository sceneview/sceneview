- **Android demo: a screen is "ready" once its models are on it, and a failed load says so** (#4459, #4460) — Secondary Camera, Camera & Gestures, Lighting, Lighting Lab, Fog, Materials and Splat Preview named their viewport "Scene ready" after the first presented frames, so TalkBack and the render tests were told the scene was there while the stage was still empty. The signal now waits for the model instances, their textures, the environment and the decoded splat, then for the frame that carries them to be drawn. A model, environment or splat that never loads shows a "Part of this scene could not load" card on the stage instead of an empty one; Secondary Camera no longer swallows the error. The camera-permission card is no longer announced as "Scene ready".
