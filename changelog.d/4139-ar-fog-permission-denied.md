- **Android demo: AR demos no longer sit on a black "Scanning for surfaces…" screen when the camera permission is denied ([#4139](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4139)).** Every screen that opens the camera — the AR demos, the camera phase of Tap to place, Rerun's live capture and room view, and the AR tab — now says the camera is off and offers to ask again or, once Android stops asking, a button to the app's system settings. Coming back with the permission granted starts the demo on its own. Screens that need no camera (Rerun's replays, the model chooser, the XR poses) never ask for it.
