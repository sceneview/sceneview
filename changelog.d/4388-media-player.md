- **A video no longer freezes the frame it is prepared in, and no longer fails in silence ([#4388](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4388)).** `rememberMediaPlayer` called the blocking `MediaPlayer.prepare()` during composition and turned every failure into `null`, so a missing asset or an unsupported codec made `VideoNode(videoPath = …)` render nothing without a word. The player is now prepared with `prepareAsync()`; the node appears once the video is ready; a failure reaches the app through the new `onError` of `VideoNode(videoPath = …)` and is logged once; the asset descriptor is closed; `isLooping` and `autoPlay` are applied when they change, so `autoPlay = isPlaying` is a play/pause button; and `videoPath` accepts an `https://`, `file://` or `content://` location as well as an asset path. **Breaking (both APIs are `@ExperimentalSceneViewApi`):** `rememberMediaPlayer(context, assetFileLocation, isLooping, autoStart): MediaPlayer?` becomes `rememberMediaPlayer(fileLocation, isLooping, autoPlay): MediaPlayerState` — `Preparing`, `Ready(player)` or `Failed(cause)` — with no deprecated overload; see the [migration guide](https://sceneview.github.io/migration/#sceneview-453x-to-4540-android-remembermediaplayer-returns-a-mediaplayerstate).
