- **Android demo: Rolling Balls tilts smoothly, further, and the balls really roll.** Dragging the board no longer loses moves or jumps: each frame the board eases towards your finger, and the balls roll down the slope you see on that same frame. The board now tips up to 35° (was 20°), in the direction you drag even after you turn the camera around it. Balls roll like solid spheres instead of sliding through syrup, and a ball pressed against a rail or another ball stays still instead of shivering. The board is new too: a lacquered maple field in a rounded walnut frame, with chrome, glass and bright rubber balls on it, soft shadows, and a floor under it. Tap the board to drop a ball where you tapped.
