- **iOS and macOS demo app: usage statistics and crash reports now wait for your consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland.** On first launch there, a sheet over Home asks "Help improve SceneView Demo" with two equal choices, **Share** and **Don't share**; swiping it away counts as no. Until you say yes, Firebase is not even started, so nothing is sent, unless you already turned notifications on: then it starts for them with collection off, after clearing the collection settings an older build left in Firebase's own storage, the same as on Android. Outside these regions nothing changes: collection stays on, with the opt-out in **About › Privacy & notifications**. That switch is also where you change your answer later; turning it off resets the analytics ID and deletes unsent crash reports. The notification pre-prompt never comes in the same session as the consent. Automation passes `-telemetry_consent granted|denied|ask` (`ask` asks wherever the device is).
