- **iOS and macOS demo app: usage statistics and crash reports now wait for your consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland.** On first launch there, a sheet over Home asks "Help improve SceneView Demo" with two equal choices, **Share** and **Don't share**; swiping it away counts as no. Until you say yes, Firebase is not even started, so nothing is sent; that holds for updates too, where an older build had left collection switched on in Firebase's own storage. Outside these regions nothing changes: collection stays on, with the opt-out in **About › Privacy & notifications**. That switch is also where you change your answer later; turning it off resets the analytics ID and deletes unsent crash reports. The notification pre-prompt never comes in the same session as the consent. Automation passes `-telemetry_consent granted|denied|ask` (`ask` asks wherever the device is).
