- **Android demo: Room Scan gives the screen back to the scene ([#4379](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4379)).** The replay left the room under half of the window and a scan in progress left the camera a sixth of it: a figures card, a corner camera card or picture-in-picture and a tall timeline card all stood on the scene. The replay now keeps one thin timeline bar just above the dock (play, filmstrip scrubber, clock), and a tap on the room hides it with the header and the dock. Everything that is read once moved into the settings sheet: the layers and their figures, the room's size, Points | Surface, a scan's counts and the computer stream's status. A scan in progress says one line (Scanning, the clock) over its 3D card, and on a phone held sideways the shutter no longer sits on that card.
