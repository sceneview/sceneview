- **iOS demo app: Cosmos' Star scene gains the ringed world and tap-to-fly camera ([#4207](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4207)).** As on Android (#4192), the scene opens on the whole system: a planet with translucent rings orbits the blue star, lit by it, with a soft day/night terminator, an atmosphere rim, the rings' shadow on its bands, the planet's shadow across the rings and a fading orbit trail. Tap the planet or the star and the camera flies there on the design system's `ease-expressive` curve, round the star rather than through it; tap again or tap empty space to pull back. The light is computed on the device's CPU into textures, refreshed in the background while the planet moves.
