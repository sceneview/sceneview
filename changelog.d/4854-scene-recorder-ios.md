- **iOS: `SceneRecorder`, photos and videos of the scene without the SwiftUI chrome.** Attach it with `.sceneRecorder(_:)` on `SceneView`, `ARSceneView` or `AutoPlacementScene`: `savePhoto()` writes a full-resolution JPEG or PNG, and `startRecording()` / `stopRecording()` write an H.264 MP4 at 1080p and 30 fps by default, with an optional microphone track and an optional time limit. On `ARSceneView` the camera feed is in the file. A `CaptureOverlay` draws a watermark on every photo and video frame. A recording finishes its file when it is stopped, reaches its limit, when the view leaves the hierarchy or when the app goes to the background. The iOS demo's model viewer and AR placement gain Take photo, Record video, a share action and a one-minute limit, like Android.
