- **iOS: `SceneRecorder`, photos and videos of the scene without the SwiftUI chrome ([#4854](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4854)).** Attach it with `.sceneRecorder(_:)` on `SceneView`, `ARSceneView` or `AutoPlacementScene`: `savePhoto()` writes a full-resolution JPEG or PNG, and `startRecording()` / `stopRecording()` write an H.264 MP4 (1080p at 30 fps by default, with an optional microphone track, time limit and watermark, and the camera feed in AR). The iOS demo's model viewer and AR placement gain Take photo, Record video, a share action and a one-minute limit, like Android.
