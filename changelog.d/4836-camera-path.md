- **Keyframed camera paths.** `CameraPosePath` interpolates free camera poses (`CameraPose`: a position and a rotation) with Linear or Catmull-Rom positions, shortest-arc slerp rotations, and per-segment or whole-path easing. `poseAt(seconds)` is a pure function of time, so playback, scrubbing and a fixed-step render agree. Presets: `orbit` (framed from a bounding box and the field of view, seamless loop), `travelling` and `dolly`. `CameraPathPlayer` adds play, pause, seek, loop and speed, advanced by wall-clock frame time, and `CameraNode.applyPose` writes a pose to a camera. The existing `CameraPath` now implements the same `CameraTimeline` interface. The Android demo app gains a Camera Path screen: an orbit around an animated fox with play/pause and a scrubber.
