- **Keyframed camera paths.** `CameraPosePath` interpolates free camera poses (`CameraPose`: a position and a rotation) with Linear or Catmull-Rom positions, shortest-arc slerp rotations, and per-segment or whole-path easing. `poseAt(seconds)` is a pure function of time, so playback, scrubbing and a fixed-step render agree. Presets: `orbit` (framed from a bounding box and the field of view, seamless loop), `travelling` and `dolly`. Catmull-Rom tangents are capped so a pause, a turn back of more than 120° or a cut in the keyframes is not overshot, while gentler turns keep their speed. `CameraPathPlayer` adds play, pause, seek, loop and speed, advanced by wall-clock frame time (a looping player keeps the lap count, so a `CameraPath.tour` of 1.5 turns loops without a jump), and `CameraNode.applyPose` / `applyCameraTimeline` write a pose to a camera. The pose API is `@ExperimentalSceneViewApi` while offline rendering builds on it, and implementing `CameraTimeline` requires the same opt-in. The existing `CameraPath` now implements the same `CameraTimeline` interface. The Android demo app gains a Camera Path screen: an orbit around an animated fox with play/pause and a scrubber.
