- **3D Scan: the live 3D map is the main view ([#4816](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4816)).** In the Android demo, a room's or an area's scan now shows the room taking shape over the whole screen, with the camera in a thumbnail that swaps places with the map in one tap and can be dragged to any corner. The map's camera follows the phone over its shoulder while scanning, pulls back to the whole room when paused or finished, and turns toward a nearby missed zone. Coverage reads as brand-colour tiles where the floor is seen, and as amber hatching, the colour the result uses for a part not scanned yet, where nothing is seen yet. Every change of view is eased, and the finish screen keeps the 3D map instead of the camera.
