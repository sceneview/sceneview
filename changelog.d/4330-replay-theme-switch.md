- **Demo app: switching the system theme while a Room Scan replay is on screen no longer closes the app ([#4330](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4330)).** On Android, going from light to dark (or back) with the replay's 3D view up — the quick-settings tile, or a scheduled dark theme — aborted the app on the next frame. The view rebuilt its layers for the new theme and freed the first set's textures and materials while the scene was still drawing them. It now keeps the layers and repaints what the theme changes in place: the trail, planes, grid and axes take the new palette, and the room's dimensions are redrawn in the new ink. The Your Room preview does the same, and both views now keep one environment for their whole life — the theme repaints its skybox — and free the light's cubemap when they close, where each switch used to leave one on the GPU. The replay's textures are freed a few frames after the material that drew them, through the library's `EngineDestroyQueue`, never in the same call.
- **`SceneView` / `ARSceneView`: replacing the environment no longer reads a destroyed skybox ([#4330](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4330)).** `rememberEnvironment(key = …)` destroys the previous environment as soon as its key changes, but the scene only received the new one a dispatch later, and Filament reads the skybox it replaces: a segfault in `Scene.setSkybox` on some swaps (a dark/light toggle that changes a colour skybox, an HDR picked from a list). The scene now lets go of an environment's skybox and indirect light before the environment is destroyed, and takes the next one in the same pass.
