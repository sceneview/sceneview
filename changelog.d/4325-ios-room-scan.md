- **iOS Room Scan ([#4325](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4325), [#4117](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4117)).** The iOS demo is named Room Scan. Its replay and sample session follow the system theme, the camera's drift, entrance and inertia stop while the replay is paused (a recenter, the map view, a rotation or a layer toggle still reframe it), and the framing covers every visible layer, point clouds included, without a distant outlier zooming the room out. Map points are drawn in one themed colour instead of their recorded colours, so they hold 3:1 on the light stage.
