- **A website page shows what a room scan is good for ([#4408](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4408)).** [`use-cases.html`](https://sceneview.github.io/use-cases.html) walks through three everyday uses of the demo's room scan — keeping how a room looked and replaying it later, a first figure for how much floor a room covers, and the outline of the room for a listing — each with a picture from the demo app, what it does and does not do today, and the smallest code behind it. The same code is in `llms.txt` as three recipes: "Scan a room and replay it later", "Measure how much floor a room scan has seen" and "Draw a room's outline from a scan".
