- **Stopping a recording right after it started no longer risks a native abort ([#4860](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4860)).** `SurfaceMirrorer.stopMirroring` destroyed the mirror's swap chain without waiting for Filament's backend, which creates that swap chain later and reads the recorder's input surface when it does; a surface released as soon as `stopMirroring` returned could be gone by then, and Filament aborted in `eglSurfaceAttrib`. `stopMirroring` now waits for the backend after destroying the swap chain, up to the same one-second bound as a scene's own surface detach. Release the surface only after `stopMirroring` returns.
