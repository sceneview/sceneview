- **Two `SceneView`s on one engine keep the full three-frame destroy delay ([#4359](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4359)).** A texture or stream released by a node is destroyed three frames later, so that nothing still drawing reads it. Every `SceneView` counted its own render as one of those frames, so two views sharing an engine halved the delay and three cut it to one display frame. The views sharing an engine now count one frame per pass, whatever order they render in and whether or not one of them is paused. A single view behaves as before.
- **Materials and Cosmos samples release their textures through the engine's destroy queue ([#4360](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4360)).** The studio backdrop's cubemap and the Cosmos horizon map were destroyed on the spot. Cosmos did it while its sheet material still sampled the map, which Filament aborts on if a frame renders before the material goes.
