- **3D Scan: a room's result opens on its shell.** A room scan in the Android demo that stands as a room opens on a dollhouse: its floor and walls as flat planes textured from the scan's photos, single-sided so the walls facing you open the room as you orbit. A room that is not a rectangle — an L, a T — gets its own walls and floor rather than the box round it. The furniture is a continuous, lit surface fused again from the scan's raw depth (a 2 cm TSDF and marching cubes, off the main thread), or coloured points when the scan kept no depth. Built in a few seconds and kept with the scan, so reopening it is instant; the Photo layer stays one tap away, and export writes the furniture too.
- **3D Scan: a short, partial room scan stands on a clean floor.** A room scan that stands as no room — a 20 to 30 s walk over part of a room — opens on its surface fused from its depth, its floor laid as one slab: gaps up to 40 cm closed, holes inside it filled, stray patches dropped, its outline smoothed, drawn flat in the colours it was seen in with an edge down all round. Everything above the floor is kept as fused; only what lies below a floor ARCore found and stray scraps are dropped. Nothing is fused past 4 m of depth, and the pose guard runs again over the recording — for the shell too — so a pose ARCore teleported without the phone noticing puts nothing out of place, and the guard lets go once the pose settles. A room's contact shadow stays inside the room's own box.
- **3D Scan keeps its raw depth, so the fusion can be replayed off the phone.** A depth scan now records every depth frame it fuses — ARCore's raw depth, its confidence, the colours read for it, the lens and the camera pose — and every frame the pose guard held back, flagged as such, kept beside the session as `capture-depth.bin`. Sessions without it open as before. On the JVM, `ScanDepthReplay` runs the same fusion and pose guard over it and gives back the cloud the phone saved.
- **3D Scan: a scan with its raw depth and no room opens on its whole fused surface**, simplified past 250,000 triangles, built with a progress bar that follows the real work, kept with the scan and exported as shown. A room's finished model replaces the raw surface only when it passes a quality check, so the result is never worse than the scan.
- **3D Scan: sharper photos.** Scan photos are kept at the camera image's resolution, up to 640 px on the long side (they were 320 px), within a bounded budget.
