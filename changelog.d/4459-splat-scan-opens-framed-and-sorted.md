- **Android demo: the 3D scan opens framed and composited, in one step** (#4459) — Splat Preview drew an empty stage while the file decoded, then swapped the camera onto the scan once it was open, and lifted its loading cover without waiting for the scan's points to be sorted for that camera. On a slow renderer the scan could be seen with its translucent points blended in the wrong order, then change a few seconds later. The stage is now only drawn once the file is open, with the camera already on the scan, and the cover stays up ("Opening the scan…") until the points are sorted for the view.
