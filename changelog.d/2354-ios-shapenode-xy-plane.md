- **iOS Shape Extrude: presets now render as real shapes instead of a collapsed edge-on ribbon (#2354).** `ShapeNode` built its polygon in the **XZ** (horizontal) plane with a +Y normal, while SceneView Android's `ShapeGeometry` builds it in the **XY** plane facing the camera (+Z). With the demo's horizontal orbit camera, a horizontal star was seen nearly edge-on — the default "Star" preset rendered as a thin gold ribbon/bowtie rather than a star (and L-Shape/Arrow were unreadable). `ShapeNode` now builds the polygon in the XY plane facing +Z (flat vertices at `(x, y, 0)` with a `(0,0,1)` normal; extrusion is symmetric along Z with front/back faces at `±depth/2` and outward-wound side quads), matching Android. The ear-clipping triangulator was unchanged — it already handled the concave star/L-shape/arrow correctly; the bug was purely the build plane. `ShapeExtrudeDemo` was retuned with a gentle compound tilt so the shape reads head-on while the extrusion depth stays visible.
