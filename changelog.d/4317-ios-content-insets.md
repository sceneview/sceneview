- **iOS: `SceneView.contentInsets(_:)` keeps the subject in what a panel leaves visible ([#4317](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4317)).** Pass how far your own sheet or side panel covers each edge and the visible rectangle becomes the camera's viewport: the subject is re-centred and scaled there, the view is not resized and the camera pose is not touched, so it also works with `.autoCenterContent(false)`. It animates with the transaction that changes the insets, and a tap still lands on what is drawn. iOS twin of Android's `SceneView(contentPadding = …)`.
- **iOS: rotating the device keeps your zoom ([#4317](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4317)).** A rotation or a resize used to hand an orbited or pinched camera back to the automatic fit. The orbit distance now follows the new shape of the view and comes back exactly when you rotate back.
