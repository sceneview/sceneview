- **iOS: `SceneView.contentInsets(_:)` keeps the subject in what a panel leaves visible ([#4317](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4317)).** Pass how far your own sheet or side panel covers each edge and the visible rectangle becomes the camera's viewport: the subject is re-centred and scaled there and the view is not resized. With `.autoCenterContent(false)` the camera pose is not touched; with automatic framing on, the orbit distance follows the visible rectangle and a pinched or orbited camera keeps its zoom. It animates with the transaction that changes the insets, and a tap still lands on what is drawn, per-entity `NodeGesture` handlers included. 3D `SceneView` only, not `ARSceneView`; orbit, pan and first-person cameras. iOS twin of Android's `SceneView(contentPadding = …)`.
