- **Demo: "Museum & Space" in the Model Viewer (Android).** Four real-world scans join the HD pack: the Apollo 11 Command Module and its cut-away interior (Smithsonian, CC0), a woolly mammoth skeleton (Smithsonian, CC0) and the Perseverance rover (NASA/JPL-Caltech, insignia removed). Each opens at its real size, its thumbnail stands on the stage until the file is on the device, and each model downloads on its own: tapping its pill shows that file's size (with a mobile-data warning on a metered network), and the pill then tracks that file alone — "queued" while another one downloads. The Wi-Fi prefetch stays limited to the Flight Helmet, and picking another model while a slow load runs no longer lets the stale one land on stage. The shelf opens under Studio lighting (a lighting the user picks stays), switching between two scans releases the first before the second is read, and Perseverance holds its pose instead of playing its 23 animations at once.
