- **Support SceneView from the Android demo, through Google Play ([#4847](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4847)).** About now opens a Support sheet with three one-time amounts; one tap opens Google Play's own purchase sheet, the purchase is consumed straight away and answered with a thank-you, and nothing in the app is unlocked or gated by it. The same sheet may ask on its own, rarely: never in the first two sessions, only right after a sample was used for 30 seconds or a first AR placement, back on the home screen and never during an AR session, at most once every 14 days, not for 180 days after a contribution, and never again after "Don't ask again". When Play billing is unavailable the sheet offers Open Collective instead. Built on Play Billing 8.3.
