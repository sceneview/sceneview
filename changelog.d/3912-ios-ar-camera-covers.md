- **iOS demo: the AR Placement Reticle Preview opens straight on its 3D stage ([#3912](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3912)).** The preview draws no camera, yet it asked for camera access and then sat on "Starting camera…" for good on an iPhone, and showed "This feature isn't available on this device" on an iPad without ARKit or in the Simulator. It now opens on every device with no camera prompt.
- **iOS demo: the Debug Overlay stress test no longer turns the camera on, and its spheres are in frame ([#3912](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3912)).** Its 3D view was left on RealityKit's default camera, which on an iPhone tracks the real world through the rear camera, with no permission flow, and the single sphere of the "1" preset sat at the screen edge behind the stats card. It now stages the spheres like Android: a ruled floor under the stage sky, seen from above at an angle, every node count framed above the stats card.
- **iOS demo: closing Body Tracking stops the camera ([#3912](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3912)).** The body-tracking session kept running after the screen closed, and the next AR screen had to compete with it for the camera.
