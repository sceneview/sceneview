- **The AR placement reticle now hides once a model is placed.** `PlacementScene` used to keep its ring sliding over the floor for the whole session, on top of the model just placed. **Default change:** with the new `reticleVisibility = ReticleVisibility.AUTO` the reticle shows while the user looks for a surface, fades out over 200 ms once a model is placed — the model is then seen over the bare camera feed, and the plane grid leaves with it — and comes back on `PlacementController.requestPlacement()`. Taps still place exactly as before. `ReticleVisibility.ALWAYS` restores the old always-on cursor; `showReticle = false` still hides it. Additive API: `PlacementController.isPlacing` / `requestPlacement()` (and `clear()` re-enters placing), `rememberPlacementController()` plus a `controller` parameter to hoist it (cleared when the scene leaves the composition, since its anchors belong to that ARCore session), an `onSurfaceFound` callback invoked once when ARCore first tracks a placement plane, a `visible` parameter on `PlacementReticleVisual` and `ARSceneScope.PlacementReticle`, and `animateReticleAlpha(phase, visible)` for apps that draw their own ring. **Binary compatibility:** a library compiled against the previous `PlacementScene` keeps its old behaviour — the hidden binary overload maps `showReticle = true` to `ReticleVisibility.ALWAYS` — while code recompiled against this release gets the new `AUTO` default; pass `reticleVisibility = ReticleVisibility.ALWAYS` to keep the always-on cursor. The Android demo's AR placement card gains a Manual mode showing it, with a "Place another" button. ([#4841](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4841))
