- **Android demo: Rerun's dense scan measures heights true and colours each point from the right pixel.** ARCore aligns its raw depth with the GPU camera texture, but the scan read it with the CPU camera image's lens. On a phone with a 4:3 CPU image and a 16:9 texture, the Pixel default, that made every height a third too tall and took each point's colour from the wrong row. The depth now takes the texture's lens, colours are read along the same ray in the CPU image, and each new depth estimate is fused once, up to 100 ms behind the camera frame, never its reprojections to later frames, which smeared edges and left holes.
