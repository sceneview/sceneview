- **Geometry updates rebuild buffers when vertex counts, per-submesh index counts or attribute layouts change ([#4344](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4344)).** Same-size edits reuse buffers. Shared geometry notifies every bound node, updates primitive ranges and bounds, and defers old-buffer destruction by three rendered frames. Shape updates now forward normal, UV scale and colour.
- **Breaking:** `GeometryNode`'s primary constructor adds `mergePrimitives: Boolean` after `builderApply`; dynamic layouts support natural submeshes or one merged primitive only. `GeometryNode.setGeometry` rebinds its fixed geometry object; use `RenderableNode` to replace geometry objects. `Geometry.vertexBuffer` and `indexBuffer` are now replaceable properties with private setters: fetch them again after topology changes, and do not lend them to independently managed raw Filament renderables. Geometry bound through nodes is released after its last consumer detaches; engine geometry-destruction helpers defer destruction and retain geometry still in use. New submeshes inherit the last material. Changing primitive count on a morphed renderable is rejected; replay custom skinning configuration in `builderApply` when rebuilding procedural renderables.
