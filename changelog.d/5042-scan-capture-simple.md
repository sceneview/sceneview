- **Demo app: 3D Scan's capture is now just the camera ([#5042](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/5042)).** The camera fills the screen, what you capture appears as fine points on the world, one short instruction shows only when needed, and a single Done saves your scan and opens the result — no map, no orange overlays, no review step. The scan picker offers all four kinds again: an object, a room, a whole home (with Next room) and an outdoor area.
