- **Demo app: the home hero no longer re-zooms or wobbles, and the scroll now glides its camera onto a second subject ([#3993](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3993)).** Opening a demo or another tab disposed the dusk flight, so every return restarted it at zero and zoomed the helmet in again; the flight now resumes where it was, with the helmet already in place, and the opening plays once per launch. Device tilt no longer steers the camera: on a page scrolled with one thumb the phone tilts all the time, and the gaze wobbled while you read. The page scroll is now the camera's only input: as the hero band scrolls away, the camera glides down from the helmet onto a fox running on the valley floor, and scrolling back up returns it to the helmet. Reduced motion keeps the still opening frame.
