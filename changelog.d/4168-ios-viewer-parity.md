- **iOS demo — Model Viewer at parity with Android:**
  - **Picker cards:** the Models picker now shows the same cards as Android: the model's render over its name and a short description ("Columbia, as flown in 1969 · HD"), on a surface card with a hairline. The model on screen is outlined, and the two cards in a row share one height. The text follows the system text size, and long names wrap instead of being cut off.
  - **Dock order:** the dock now reads Models, Lighting, Animate, Recenter, as on Android.
  - **Lighting:** Museum & Space models open under the neutral Studio lighting instead of the green-tinted Chinese Garden, as long as the lighting is still the default. Going back to another model restores the garden only if the app switched to Studio itself; a lighting you picked stays. "Reset lighting" returns to the lighting the model opens under.
