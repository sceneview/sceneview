- **Demo app: three demos keep their subject clear of the controls in landscape ([#4326](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4326)).** On a phone held sideways, Double Pendulum swung behind the `Release` button, the Model Viewer's model sat half behind the dock and Contact Shadow's scene ran under its controls. The three now hand what covers the scene — the chrome, the settings sheet where it is, the display cutout — to `contentPadding`, and fit their camera for the area left free at rest: the subject is drawn in that area and follows the sheet up, and the orbit the user set survives opening Settings. The pendulum's fit now includes the tip bob and, in landscape, the stand's foot. The viewer's Lighting sheet reports the half of the window it rests at in landscape, not its whole size, so the model stays above it instead of shrinking away.
