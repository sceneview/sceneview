<!-- category: Changed -->
- **`PlacementScene` with your own `ShadowReceiverPlane`: pass `groundShadows = false` ([#4819](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4819)).** `groundShadows` defaults to `true` since #4840, so placed models already cast their shadow on the real floor once the plane grid has receded. If your `content` adds its own `ShadowReceiverPlane`, turn it off, or the two coplanar receivers darken the shadow twice.
- **Contact shadow under `AutoPlacementModel`, on by default ([#4819](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4819)).** An object placed on a floor or a table now gets a soft dark pool underneath, sized from its bounds, that turns and scales with it and stays put when the estimated light moves the real shadow to one side. Never drawn for a wall placement. Existing callers see it without changing anything; if you already place your own `ContactShadow` under the model, opt out with `AutoPlacementState.showsContactShadow = false`.

<!-- category: Fixed -->
- **A device without HDR light estimation no longer fails the AR session ([#4819](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4819)).** `ARSession.configure` steps the default `ENVIRONMENTAL_HDR` down to `AMBIENT_INTENSITY`, then to `DISABLED`, when the device does not support it, instead of letting ARCore throw `UnsupportedConfigurationException`. HDR stays the default wherever it runs.
