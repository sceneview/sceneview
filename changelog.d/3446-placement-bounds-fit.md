- **AR placement bounds fit ([#3446](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3446)).** `placementBoundsFit` checks a transformed model box against a convex plane polygon and reports containment plus signed clearance/overhang in metres. A `Plane.placementBoundsFit` adapter follows merged planes to the current tracked ARCore polygon, preserving affine transforms for tilted planes through rigid-pose inversion. Boundary contact and polygon convexity allow 10 micrometres of numerical tolerance without rounding the reported margin. Results have an internal constructor without data-class copy/component methods; the geometry core is JVM-testable and existing placement APIs are unchanged. No placement composable or app integration is included.
