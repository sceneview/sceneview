- **Android demo app: usage statistics and crash reports now wait for consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland.** On first launch there, SceneView Demo asks "Help improve SceneView Demo" with two equal choices, **Share** and **Don't share**, and Firebase does not start until the answer: no analytics, no crash report, no network call to Google before it. Closing the sheet counts as Don't share and is never asked again. The answer can be changed anytime in **About → Privacy & notifications → Share usage statistics**; turning it off resets the analytics ID and deletes unsent crash reports. Updating from an older build also clears the collection settings it left in Firebase, so nothing is sent before the answer: crash reports cached by the old build are deleted unsent. Elsewhere nothing changes (on by default, off in About). Ad signals stay denied everywhere, and the notification pre-prompt never shows in the same session as the consent question. The privacy policy states the legal basis per region and discloses the usage metrics ML Kit and AICore send Google. ([#4257](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4257))
