- **The Rerun AR replay comes to the iOS demo, and you can record your own room ([#4068](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4068)).** The demo opens on "Scan a room in 3D": **Record your room** with ARKit, watch the sample session, or open a file. Stopping a recording opens it on the same 3D replay as the sample (camera path, keyframe photos, planes and points), with 3D, Map and Camera views and a scrubbing filmstrip. Your sessions stay on the iPhone across launches until you delete them. Share any replay as `.rrd`, `.glb`, `.usdz` or `.ply`, all written on the phone. The app reads back its own `.svscan` scan files and the `.rrd` files it exports, from Files, AirDrop or its file picker. Deep link: `sceneview://demo/ar-rerun`.
