<!-- category: Fixed -->
- **Android**: `ARCoachingOverlay` is now a coaching card instead of a 96 dp glyph with a one-word caption. The card shows an illustration (a phone sweeping over a floor or a wall, lighting the feature points it finds; chevrons pointing back when the object is out of view) above a two-line instruction ("Move your phone slowly · Point it at the floor or a table"). When ARCore names a reason, a chip says it ("Too dark", "Too fast", "Low detail") and the headline becomes the fix. After 8 s without a surface, the detail suggests a better spot. "Surface found" is a pill with a springing check. The card sits above centre, out of the thumb zone, and holds each state long enough to read: a hiccup in tracking no longer flashes it. With animations off, only fades remain. In the demo app, every placement flow and the Plane Renderer V2 and Plane lifecycle demos show the card, and status pills step aside while it speaks. ([#4038](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4038))

- **Android**: `ARCoachingOverlay(guidance, contentPadding = ...)`, `ArGuidanceState.hint` / `scanLingering`, `rememberArGuidanceState(placement, surface, trackingFailureReason)`, and `rememberArGuidanceState(cameraReady =, isTracking =, surfaceFound =, trackingFailureReason =)` for scenes without auto-placement — name those arguments, three booleans in a row swap silently. `AutoPlacementScene(coachingContentPadding = ...)` keeps the card and its "Surface found" pill above your own bottom chrome; in a short landscape window the pill now rests on that band instead of dropping onto it. - **Android: `PlacementScene(coaching = true)` shows the `ARCoachingOverlay` card instead of `PlaneDiscoveryGuide`, and the `sceneview_coaching_word_scan`, `sceneview_coaching_word_paused` and `sceneview_coaching_word_look_back` strings are removed** with the glyph that used them. `coachingBottomClearance` now clears the card. The stateless overload grows to `ARCoachingOverlay(cue, surface, modifier, hint, scanLingering, contentPadding)` — `modifier` stays the first optional parameter, so existing calls compile and binaries compiled against v4.51 keep linking. **To migrate:** to keep the `PlaneDiscoveryGuide` look in `PlacementScene`, pass `coaching = false` and compose `PlaneDiscoveryGuide` over the scene yourself; replace any reference to a `sceneview_coaching_word_*` string with your own resource. ([#4038](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4038))
