- **Android**: `ARCoachingOverlay` is now a coaching card instead of a 96 dp glyph with a one-word caption. The card shows an illustration (a phone sweeping over a floor or a wall, lighting the feature points it finds; chevrons pointing back when the object is out of view) above a two-line instruction ("Move your phone slowly · Point it at the floor or a table"). When ARCore names a reason, a chip says it ("Too dark", "Too fast", "Low detail") and the headline becomes the fix. After 8 s without a surface, the detail suggests a better spot. "Surface found" is a pill with a springing check. The card sits above centre, out of the thumb zone, and holds each state long enough to read: a hiccup in tracking no longer flashes it. With animations off, only fades remain. New API: `ARCoachingOverlay(guidance, contentPadding = ...)`, `ArGuidanceState.hint` / `scanLingering`, `rememberArGuidanceState(placement, surface, trackingFailureReason)`, and `rememberArGuidanceState(cameraReady, isTracking, surfaceFound, trackingFailureReason)` for scenes without auto-placement. `PlacementScene` uses the card instead of `PlaneDiscoveryGuide`. In the demo app, every placement flow and the Plane Renderer V2 and Plane lifecycle demos show the card, and status pills step aside while it speaks. ([#4038](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4038))
