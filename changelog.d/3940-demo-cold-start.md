- **Demo app: faster first frame and no blank home hero on cold start ([#3940](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/3940)).** The home hero's 3D stack is now composed one frame after the app's first frame instead of inside it. On the emulator with a release build, time to first frame went from a median of 233 ms to 146 ms (p90 from 254 ms to 151 ms). The hero also keeps its still image until the helmet's first textured frame is actually presented. Before, the card showed empty and black for about 1.7 s while the GPU finished its first draw. `tools/measure-demo-cold-start.sh` measures both points.
