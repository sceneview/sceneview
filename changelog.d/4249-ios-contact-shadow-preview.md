- **iOS demo app: Contact Shadow Preview ([#4249](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4249)).** The Android demo comes to iOS under the same id and deep link. Two identical boxes stand in a neutral room: the left one bounces onto a soft contact shadow that tightens and darkens as it lands, the right one hovers with none. A wall TV sits on its own shadow, with a Floor / Wall / Table preset picker and a one-line verdict on the stage. Shadows toggles from the dock; the sheet holds contact shadows, bounce motion and a 0–1.5× intensity slider. The shadow is a procedural quad with the falloff of Android's `contact_shadow.mat`, because RealityKit's `GroundingShadowComponent` has no intensity and cannot fall on a wall.
