- **Demo app: Matter's steel and gel read as what they are, and switching rarely hitches ([#5140](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/5140)).** Brushed steel now shows bright reflections and visible brushing lines instead of looking like black plastic. The gel is clear with a light aqua tint and casts a caustic glow on the floor instead of looking dark brown. The studio spotlight and the contact shadow now sit under the solid instead of drifting with the camera. A one-time hint ("Press and hold · Swipe to change material") is shown until the first press. Each material is drawn once in the background whenever the stage has been still for a moment, so switching to a material that has been warmed this way plays without a pause.
