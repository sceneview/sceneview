- Keep AR plane marks at fixed world-space dimensions, clip their feather to the tracked polygon, and promptly remove paused or subsumed planes. Plain ARSceneView and ARScene now request supported AUTOMATIC depth for their plane marks by default; an explicit depth mode (including DISABLED) or planeDepthOcclusion=false wins, while model occlusion stays under app control. Placement scenes enable supported camera-depth occlusion, fade guides after placement or dragging, and leave no dark footprint ring or artificial shadow pool by default ([#5322](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5322), [#5323](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5323), [#5324](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5324), [#5321](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5321)).
