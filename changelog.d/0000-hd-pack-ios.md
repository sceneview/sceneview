- **iOS demo — HD pack:** heavy demo assets now ship outside the App Store build. They are downloaded once, verified by SHA-256 and kept in Application Support (excluded from iCloud backup) from the content-addressed `hd-pack-v1` GitHub Release, listed in the bundled `assets/hd-pack/ios.json` manifest. The download starts on its own only on an unmetered network. On cellular or Low Data Mode, nothing downloads until the user taps "Download now", which states the size first. The About tab gets an "HD scenes" row (size, Downloaded / Download now / Remove), and a scene that needs an HD asset shows its bundled stand-in right away with an "HD · downloading 34 %" pill, then swaps in the HD asset. First asset: the Khronos **Flight Helmet** (CC0, 2K textures, 52 MB) in the Model Viewer, with the Damaged Helmet as its stand-in. `tools/convert-usdz.sh` now maps glTF `KHR_materials_transmission` to alpha-blended opacity, because UsdPreviewSurface has no transmission and glass would otherwise render opaque.
