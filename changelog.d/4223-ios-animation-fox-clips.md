- **The Animation demo opens on the same fox on Android and iOS.** iOS used to open on a cyberpunk character with play/pause and a speed slider. It now opens on the Khronos fox, like Android, and has the same controls: the Survey / Walk / Run clip picker, a card showing the clip, Playing or Paused, and time / duration, play/pause, scrub, speed, and Loop / Once. The iOS Soldier is now Android's soldier, and it walks. The old iOS character held a one-frame pose. In dark mode the fox now stands on the dark stage on both platforms, not on a pale grey backdrop. The dark Home card for Animation now shows the fox on that dark stage, not a copy of the light card.
- **The AR Placement card on Home shows the Toy Car, the model the demo places, on Android and iOS.** It used to show a fox the demo has not placed for a long time. The light and dark cards stand the Toy Car on a real living-room floor at its true size, with no placement ring, since the demo places the model on its own.
