- **Demo: WebP-textured models now render textured (#2305).** `shiba.glb` and `threejs_soldier.glb` embedded their base-color textures as WebP (`EXT_texture_webp`), which rendered as untextured/white because Filament's Android prebuilt ships `gltfio` with WebP support compiled out (`isWebpSupported() == false`, no `image/webp` provider registered — verified at the binary level). The two demo assets are re-encoded to PNG (JPEG textures preserved, Draco geometry re-applied) so they load through Filament's default `StbProvider` and render correctly. General SDK-level WebP support is tracked separately on #2305 (needs a Filament build with libwebp, or a transcode pipeline — no Kotlin `TextureProvider` hook exists in the pinned binding).
