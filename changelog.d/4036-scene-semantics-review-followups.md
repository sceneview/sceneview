- **Scene Semantics: the overlay material is now loaded and drawn by a test ([#4036](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4036)).** `semantics_overlay.filamat` had no caller in the repository, so the fix that put its palette back in ARCore's order was only ever read. `SemanticsOverlayRenderTest` loads it on every instrumented run and, where pixel readback works, draws the bytes 0, 1, 11 and 12 and checks they come out transparent, sky blue, cyan and transparent. The byte ARCore writes is compared with the SDK's own `nativeCode` for all twelve classes, the demo's eleven colours are written out by class name, and the demo's palette is checked against the material's (PERSON's pink was 4 in 255 apart on one channel; the demo's now matches).
- **The render tests read their pixels back on a phone.** On a Pixel 4a the readback never completed: the frame that carried it was skipped whenever Filament advised skipping one, and its callback had nowhere to run. 41 of the 44 render tests timed out there; 36 now pass, and the 8 that fail on their own assertions are listed in [#5100](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5100).
