- **Android demo: 3D Scan captures on its live model ([#5048](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5048)).** While you scan, the growing model, in the colours the camera saw, fills the screen on a dark stage with a floor grid and its live width and depth. The camera slides into a small viewfinder beside a recorder (red Finish button, Pause / Resume) and one guidance line. Object, Room, Home and Outdoor scans are kept.
- **The phone stands in the model.** Its view cone and the path it walked are drawn in the live model. The view follows from behind and above, eased, framed on the model's own reach so a turn never moves it nearer or further, and steps back as the room grows. The model's points are drawn finer (2 cm), so a wall seen well reads as a surface and one seen thinly as scattered points.
- **One indication of where to go.** The guidance line names the side of the nearest gap in the floor ("Turn left, nothing scanned there yet") only for a gap worth going back for, at least 0.5 m² and surrounded by floor already scanned, never a notch of the model's outline, once that gap has stayed empty for 3 s, and holds it for at least 4.5 s or until the gap is filled. Only that gap is hatched on the model, and the hatching and the line always change together. "Raise your phone to the walls" is said once the phone has stayed off them for 5 s, not at every sweep.
- **A first scan says what to film, where to move and when it is finished.** The red button's ring fills as the phone turns: twice as thick as a plain shutter ring, shaded on both edges so it reads over a white wall as over a dark room, and swelling for a beat at each step further. The line says it in plain words: "Film the floor and the walls, slowly", "Keep turning slowly until the circle is full", then "The circle is full. Tap the red button to finish", with a haptic, once the turn is full. Walking round an object fills the same ring. Outdoors, and round an object that cannot be walked round, the line asks instead ("Got it all? Tap the red button to finish"). The red button finishes nothing in a capture's first three seconds.
- **A cupboard or shelf is a new kind of scan.** "What are you scanning?" offers "A cupboard or shelf" (a niche, a gap, the cupboard under a sink or a car boot too), filmed standing in front of its opening; its capture and the scan it keeps are called "Cupboard or shelf" too, with the time and no floor area, which says nothing of a cupboard. Its result says the inside as width × depth × height in centimetres (inches to the half inch in US English), how many things are in the way, and the biggest box that rests on the bottom and slides in from the opening clear of all of it; with no bottom filmed it asks for it instead, and with no top seen it says so ("No top seen. If it has one, scan again and tilt up to it"). The inside, each thing in the way and the box that fits are drawn on the model, through its walls. The space measured is the one the phone aimed at, so a cupboard among kitchen units is told from the wall above its worktop. Export adds "Inside measurements — JSON": one object in metres, in the space's own frame (right-handed, Y up, origin at the back-left-bottom corner), with the inside box, the faces left open, each obstacle and the box that fits, for whoever designs what goes in. The capture asks for one face at a time, each with the move that films it ("Point straight at the back of it", "Now the bottom: tilt your phone down", then each side and the top). A face is done once the live scan holds it, or after eight seconds for a shelf with no sides or a crate with no top; the ring fills a fifth per face held, and the scan looks complete once it holds its back and its bottom. A phone that walks off is told at once ("Stay in front of the opening. For a whole room, go back and choose A room"), and a scan whose phone went further than 1.2 m from where it started gets no figure at all ("This is more than one cupboard or shelf") rather than the measure of nothing.
- **An object scan stays an object's.** Under "An object" the capture no longer counts square metres of floor nor writes the floor's width and depth. A phone that leaves the object for the room is told at once ("Stay on one object. For a whole room, go back and choose A room"). The result finds the object the phone went round, draws its box on the model, frames it, and says its length × depth × height in centimetres (inches to the half inch in US English). The box is of all that stands there, from the floor to its top: a chair whose seat the scan saw, and nothing of the column under it, is not boxed as a seat hanging over the floor. When the phone went round no single object it says so ("No single object stands out") and how to scan one, rather than a size.
- **A room's figures say what they span.** A kept room opened again draws the same two dimensions as one just scanned; it used to draw none. Until the walls the scan found close the same room, a card says "Not the room's size yet" and what to film ("film every wall from the floor up, all the way round").
- **An outdoor scan that came out in pieces is shown as its points.** When less than half of the ground scanned became a surface, the result stays on the points the scan took, says so, and names its span and its ground as what was scanned, not as the place's size. Model stays one tap away.
- **A result stays framed above its cards.** A card that arrives under the model once the result is shown used to leave the model low against it, or off to one side of the screen: the camera let go of its framing when its opening move ended, and a room's framing could switch to the side beside the one on screen. The camera now keeps following its framing, from the side the result was shown on. The box drawn round an object or inside a cupboard is drawn whole, also where the scan has a hole behind it.
- **A scan held off a jumped pose says the ways out.** When ARCore's pose jumps and does not come back within 4 s, the line stops asking to go back and names the red button (keep this scan) and Back (restart).
- **Phones without raw depth keep their points.** The sparse points, in the colours the camera saw, are drawn over the camera again, and the line says that this phone captures photos and points without a dense surface.
- **Finishing keeps the model on screen.** The result opens on the capture's last picture, names its two steps ("Building the surface · step 1 of 2", "Cleaning up walls and floor · step 2 of 2", or "Cleaning up the surface" for an object and outdoors) and stays still until a gesture.
- **Room and home scans are rebuilt as a bounded 5 cm screened Poisson surface before photo texturing.** Object and outdoor scans keep fused detail, surfaces a single depth frame saw are no longer dropped, and unobserved regions remain open.
