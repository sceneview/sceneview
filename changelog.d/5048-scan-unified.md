- **Android demo: 3D Scan captures on its live model ([#5048](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/5048)).** While you scan, the growing model, in the colours the camera saw, fills the screen on a dark stage with a floor grid and its live width and depth. The camera slides into a small viewfinder beside a recorder (red Finish button, Pause / Resume) and one guidance line. Object, Room, Home and Outdoor scans are kept.
- **The phone stands in the model.** Its view cone and the path it walked are drawn in the live model. The view follows from behind and above, eased, framed on the model's own reach so a turn never moves it nearer or further, and steps back as the room grows. The model's points are drawn finer (2 cm), so a wall seen well reads as a surface and one seen thinly as scattered points.
- **One indication of where to go.** The guidance line names the side of the nearest gap in the floor ("Turn left, nothing scanned there yet") only for a gap worth going back for, at least 0.5 m² and surrounded by floor already scanned, never a notch of the model's outline, once that gap has stayed empty for 3 s, and holds it for at least 4.5 s or until the gap is filled. Only that gap is hatched on the model, and the hatching and the line always change together.
- **A scan held off a jumped pose says the ways out.** When ARCore's pose jumps and does not come back within 4 s, the line stops asking to go back and names the red button (keep this scan) and Back (restart).
- **Phones without raw depth keep their points.** The sparse points, in the colours the camera saw, are drawn over the camera again, and the line says that this phone captures photos and points without a dense surface.
- **Finishing keeps the model on screen.** The result opens on the capture's last picture, names its two steps ("Building the surface · step 1 of 2", "Cleaning up walls and floor · step 2 of 2") and stays still until a gesture.
- **Room and home scans are rebuilt as a bounded 5 cm screened Poisson surface before photo texturing.** Object and outdoor scans keep fused detail, surfaces a single depth frame saw are no longer dropped, and unobserved regions remain open.
