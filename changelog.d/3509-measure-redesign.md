- **Demo: Measure is rebuilt from scratch around three modes — Distance, Wall and Room ([#3509](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3509)).** A centre reticle snaps to placed points, plane corners and edges, and to vertical and level guides, with a haptic tick and a chip naming the lock. Segments draw themselves and carry camera-facing labels sized by distance, in metric or imperial by locale. Wall mode takes the two ends of a wall on the floor, then raises a translucent wall to the aimed height; Room mode traces the floor (corners square themselves), closes it, and raises every wall to the ceiling, with floor area, perimeter and wall area. Undo steps back one action at a time, and Share sends the dimensions as text. The geometry, snapping, undo flow, units and share text are pure Kotlin under unit tests; accuracy on a real phone is still to be measured (`needs-device`).
