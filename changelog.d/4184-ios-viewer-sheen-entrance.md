- **iOS demo — Model Viewer**: models fly in the way they do on Android. When a model opens, the camera starts further out, 24° to the side and a little higher, then glides to the resting view in 700 ms while the model rises into place. Recenter glides back from wherever you left the camera. Touching the model during the flight hands the camera to you at once, and Reduce Motion skips the flight. The Toy Car's cloth is copper velvet again, as on Android, instead of dull pink: its USDZ had lost the glTF sheen and base colour.
- **SceneViewSwift**: a `cameraPose(_:)` write that moves the camera now takes it from the fit-to-bounds pass, as a drag does, so a pose animated frame by frame is no longer pulled back to the fit while the content's bounds settle. A `contentID(_:)` swap or a `recenterCamera(_:)` hands the camera back.
