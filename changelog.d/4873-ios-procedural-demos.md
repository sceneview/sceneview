- **iOS: `TubeNode` and `MeshNode.wireframe`, and procedural demos on par with Android ([#4873](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4873)).** `TubeNode(points:radius:radialSegments:closed:caps:material:)` sweeps a tube along a polyline and rebuilds in place with `update(points:radius:)`. `MeshNode.wireframe(of:thickness:hideQuadDiagonals:material:)` draws the edges of any mesh as thin prisms, because RealityKit has no wireframe mode; the width is in metres. The iOS demo's Geometry Primitives (torus, capsule, wireframe, material sliders), Lines & Paths (curve kinds, stroke, control points, moving marker), Custom Geometry (live knot sliders, wireframe) and Performance Test (stress ramp to 2 000 spheres with frame stats and verdict) now follow Android.
