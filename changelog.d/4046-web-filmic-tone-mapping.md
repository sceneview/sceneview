- **The `sceneview-web` Kotlin/JS bundle tone-maps with Filmic, like Android ([#4046](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4046)).** The view was left on Filament's default ACES (legacy) curve while Android sets Filmic (`SceneFactories.createView`), so one model was graded differently on the two platforms. Every scene rendered by the Kotlin/JS bundle (`sceneview-web.js`, npm `sceneview-web`) changes: saturated colours keep more of their saturation instead of washing out, at about the same brightness. The hand-written `website-static/js/sceneview.js` is aligned separately ([#4347](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4347)).
