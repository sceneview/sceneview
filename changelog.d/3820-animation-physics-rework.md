- **Android demo: Animation & Physics reworked so every control does something you can see ([#3820](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3820), [#3826](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/3826)).** Physics is now a tray of rubber, steel and foam balls. Each material bounces, weighs and rolls differently, with mass-weighted contacts. The material chips, Drop, Tilt and Reset sit on the scene instead of inside the settings sheet. Reset restores the opening shot without rebuilding the scene, so it can no longer flash black. Dropped balls start inside the frame, and the rails hold them at any height, so a ball stays visible after it lands. The camera frames the tray in the band above the controls and can no longer orbit under it. The streamed vases and barrels, which rode invisible sphere colliders, are gone from this screen (the assets stay in the catalogue). Animation opens on the bundled fox, grounded and framed from its measured bounds in a garden environment, with a slow turntable. The soldier is still available. Double Pendulum opens centred on its hinge, with the whole swing in frame, and Release is now on the scene.
