- **Demo app: Matter (#5002).** A tactile material studio at the top of Play & feel. Six samples are shaded with physically based materials: memory foam, clear lightly tinted gel, velvet, leather, Carrara marble and brushed steel, grounded on a lit studio surface. Each one answers with its own haptic pattern, driven by the simulated rebound. Soft samples form a smooth finger well with a gentle raised rim: memory foam keeps the trace and recovers slowly, gel transmits the floor and springs back, and velvet retains its grazing sheen on a flat resting surface. Marble and steel stay rigid. The demo opens with the SceneView cube dropping into the foam, which is skipped on first touch, with reduced motion and in QA builds. Deep links: `sceneview://demo/matter` (alias `feel`). No public SDK API change.
