- **Matter** — a tactile Android material studio with one rounded hero sample: memory foam, amber refractive gel, velvet, pebbled leather, Carrara marble or brushed steel. Baked rest-space PBR finishes stay attached while GPU deformation bends the studio lighting into the finger's indentation. The theme-aware Model Viewer showroom supplies HDR lighting, a reflective floor and contact shadows. Filter chips and empty-space flings select materials; `feel`/`matter` deep links, recording and lifecycle cancellation remain supported. No public SDK API changes.
- Give each soft sample a distinct rim and drag response: foam follows over 130 ms and leaves a fading groove, gel over 55 ms with a tighter rim, velvet over 35 ms with eighteen folds and a crushed-pile sheen trail, and leather over 25 ms with seven folds. Eight uniform contact samples bound each trail; analytic gradients deform normals without vertex uploads. Foam has a 140 ms release plateau and slow memory; gel rebounds through rest and sends a damped release wave. Rigid samples retain their sub-millimetre bounce and primary-colour hairline ripple.
- Restore clear honey-amber gel through thickness-dependent Beer–Lambert absorption (linear RGB coefficients 0.08/1.25/5.5, optical path up to 0.95 model units, tapering at the bevel). Keep the white dielectric interface, 1.45 IOR, 0.045 roughness and two suspended bubble layers. Retain v8's disabled opaque self-shadow/SSAO and 8% contact shadow; compression changes the optical thickness without a darkening term.
- Give Carrara a whole-slab cloudy blue-grey haze, two broad feathered seams (0.22–0.46-unit Gaussian widths) and eighteen meandering secondary streams (0.018–0.045 units). Bake three neighbouring streams per texel sample, with continuous fixed centres, a box prefilter and mip filtering. Mineral colour blends toward linear 0.36/0.41/0.47, capped at 72%, to avoid black cracks. Keep the finer low-contrast foam cells and v8's rounded leather grain.
- Report the shader's visual peak in scene millimetres (0.025 units/mm), rounded by the existing caption. A 2.4 mm spring displacement in foam now reports about 14 mm; 1 mm in leather reports 3 mm. A bounded 49×29 search, only during shallow foam contact with deeper old grooves, includes the shader's trail union. Untouched recovery below 2 physical mm retains the character caption.
- Drive haptics from spring state and actual pointer travel, retaining distinct contact/drag/release signatures, primitive capability checks, rate caps and cancellation. Remap only waveform amplitudes to 25–80% for nonzero segments, preserving envelope ordering, pauses and durations. Composition scales remain unchanged; the opening adds one supported THUD or the strongest waveform fallback. QA and system-disabled haptics stay silent.
- Stage the polished dark ceramic SceneView cube with a 400 ms gravity fall, 120 ms deep wide compression (12 mm minimum spring depth, 1.65-unit well radius), 400 ms rest and 900 ms slow lift. Supported whole-top compression briefly squashes the foam; its slow recovery follows the lift. Size the travel to exit the viewport before hiding the cube; finish the opening at 2.22 s, skip on first touch, reduced motion or QA mode. Material changes use the 450 ms container motion token: the outgoing sample slides/lower by 0.14 units with a fading shadow, then the incoming sample rises through the floor with 0.28-unit lateral travel, a material-specific settle, gel wobble/slow foam recovery or a crisp rigid ripple. Reduced motion and QA switch instantly.
- Raise camera elevation from 24° to 30°, retain the 48° horizontal FOV and approximately 90% portrait width, and centre the silhouette around 48% of free-stage height with room for its shadow. After four untouched seconds, orbit ±6° over twelve seconds with a two-second ease-in; freeze the current camera pose immediately on touch so picking stays aligned. Reduced motion and QA disable the loop. Reuse the existing main-thread frame callback and uniform-only deformation; update the camera only when its pose or viewport changes.
- **v10 performance:** replace the opaque all-features material with plain-lit foam, sheen-only velvet, coat-only leather/marble and anisotropic steel. All six materials share `matter_deformation.vs`; compile-time family switches remove rigid deformation, unrelated folds, trails and release waves. Inactive trail slots skip their exponential, folds stop below 0.0001 model units, and supported bottom vertices skip deformation.
- Bake v9's Worley, nap, Carrara and brushing fields once per loaded family on `Dispatchers.Default`, with scanline cancellation; upload RG8 atlases and mipmaps on the main thread. Each 1024×512 atlas gives its hero top 512² texels, separate padded rounded-side charts, four samples per texel and ~1.33 MiB of GPU storage including mips. Five loaded opaque families total ~6.67 MiB. One filtered lookup replaces the 27-cell search or noise/vein loops per fragment; gel retains its view-dependent bubble parallax and absorption. A cold family waits for its bake before entering; initial readiness also waits for foam detail. Atlases are destroyed with the sample.
- Keep the 128×64 soft top and six bevel subdivisions, reduce side height/bottom density (23,062 → 18,626 vertices, 19.2% less), and use 4,178 vertices for rigid samples (81.9% less). Top and side edge subdivisions still match while deforming. Matter disables SSAO, uses PCF shadows, retains both showroom contact-shadow layers and reduces the mirror target from 25% to 12.5% per axis (75% fewer mirror pixels). Actual mirror submissions are capped at 30 Hz during action, 15 Hz during the 30 Hz orbit, and cached when unchanged.
- Target 60 Hz during contact/opening/recovery/transitions, 30 Hz for orbit, and park on demand when still (including QA/reduced motion). One cancellable idle deadline wakes the orbit; touch, focus/resume, texture uploads and view changes wake rendering. Enable homogeneous dynamic resolution between 67% and 100% with medium-quality upsampling (up to 55.1% fewer main-view pixels under GPU load). The scaler targets the active display interval, doubled during orbit. Debug-only `MatterPerf` logs once per accumulated second of presented `onFrame` intervals: material, cadence, average milliseconds, fps and interval count; timing resets across parking/lifecycle/cadence changes. This is presentation-callback timing, not a GPU timer or SurfaceFlinger measurement.
- Expected largest GPU gains are removal of unused lighting lobes and replacement of procedural fragment ALU with one texture fetch; their individual millisecond savings require on-device A/B measurement. Quantified reductions above are work counts, not measured GPU time. Visual trades needing the lead's phone-distance comparison: prefiltered micro-detail/fine veins, possible atlas mip seams, softer low-resolution reflection, firmer PCF penumbrae, less cavity darkening without SSAO and dynamic-resolution softness. Gel colour/IOR, finish constants, top deformation, opening and haptics are retained. The 60 fps target / 30 fps floor and visual equivalence remain unverified; no device, emulator, adb or Gradle was used.
