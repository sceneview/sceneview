- **Matter** — an Android tactile material sample with themed, solid foam, oak and steel blocks, a lattice that compresses across the top and front section, live plain-language readouts, and material-specific haptics. A primitive SceneView mark briefly loads the foam using the same spring-damper as touch; the opening respects reduced motion and QA still mode and cancels on touch. Foam retains its dent, oak emits grain ticks only during travel, and steel responds with one light ripple. The retired `feel` deep link redirects to `matter`. Physics, pressure inference, mesh deformation, opening and haptic scheduling remain JVM-testable; this sample does not add a public cross-platform SDK API.
