- **Matter** — a tactile Android material studio with one rounded hero sample: memory foam, refractive gel, velvet, leather, Carrara marble or brushed steel. Procedural PBR finishes stay attached to the surface while GPU deformation bends the studio lighting into the finger's indentation. Memory foam recovers slowly after a short plateau, gel rebounds through rest, and rigid samples acknowledge contact with a small bounce and a primary-colour hairline ripple. The theme-aware Model Viewer showroom supplies HDR lighting, a reflective floor and contact shadows, complemented by a soft key shadow. Filter chips and empty-space flings switch samples with the app's spring; reduced motion and QA mode switch instantly. A polished ceramic SceneView cube opens the experience by loading the foam, without logo brackets. The `feel` and `matter` deep links, recording, lifecycle cancellation and existing haptic patterns remain supported. Physics, rounded geometry, tangent frames, picking, framing and caption resources have JVM tests. This demo adds no public SDK API.
- Give each soft sample a raised rim and a distinct drag response: memory foam follows over 130 ms and leaves a fading groove, gel over 55 ms with a tighter, taller rim, velvet over 35 ms with eighteen soft radial folds and a crushed-pile sheen trail, and leather over 25 ms with seven crisp folds. Eight uniform contact samples bound each trail; analytic displacement gradients keep the normals attached without per-frame vertex uploads. Foam retains its 140 ms recovery plateau, while gel rebounds through three diminishing upward peaks and sends a damped wave across its top. Marble and steel retain their sub-millimetre contact bounce and hairline ripple.
- Replace the foam's dark speckles with finer, shallow, low-contrast cells on yellowed cream; give polished Carrara one dominant diagonal vein flow with gentle meanders and branching. Bound the gel's absorption path and remove its opaque self-shadow while retaining the showroom contact shadow. A closer 48° horizontal-FOV camera at 24° elevation frames about 90% of portrait width, with the silhouette near the free stage's centre and its top near the upper third. Captions whose depth rounds to zero always show the material's character, including after the opening and during gel zero crossings. JVM coverage includes drag lag, trail expiry, release peaks, captions and the closer camera's grazing-side picking; both material packages are rebuilt with Filament 1.77.3.
