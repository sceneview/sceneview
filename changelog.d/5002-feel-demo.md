- **Matter** — an Android tactile material sample with larger three-quarter perspective foam, oak and steel slabs, crisp edges using the existing Cosmos lattice material, compact captions, and material-specific haptics. An amplified finger-following bowl compresses foam across the top and visible sections; oak keeps a small dent and steel a tiny ring, while the readouts retain simulated millimetres. A primitive SceneView mark briefly loads the foam using the same spring-damper as touch; the opening respects reduced motion and QA still mode and cancels on touch. Foam retains its dent, oak emits grain ticks only during travel, and steel responds with one light ripple. The retired `feel` deep link redirects to `matter`. Physics, pressure inference, mesh deformation, opening and haptic scheduling remain JVM-testable; this sample does not add a public cross-platform SDK API.
