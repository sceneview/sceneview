- **Matter** — an Android tactile material sample with larger three-quarter perspective foam, oak and steel slabs, crisp edges using the existing Cosmos lattice material, coherent blue foam, warm role-based oak and contrasting steel bodies, compact captions, and material-specific haptics. Presses anywhere on a slab’s silhouette map to the nearest top-face point and follow finger travel. An amplified finger-following bowl compresses foam across the top and visible sections; oak keeps a small dent and steel a tiny ring, while the readouts retain simulated millimetres. A primitive SceneView mark resized to fit the foam row with a camera-facing frame and matching short descent/lift briefly loads the foam using the same spring-damper as touch; the opening respects reduced motion and QA still mode and cancels on touch. Foam retains its dent, oak emits grain ticks only during travel, and steel responds with one light ripple. The retired `feel` deep link redirects to `matter`. Physics, pressure inference, mesh deformation, opening and haptic scheduling remain JVM-testable; this sample does not add a public cross-platform SDK API.
