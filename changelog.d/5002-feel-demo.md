- **Matter** — a tactile Android material studio with one rounded hero sample: memory foam, refractive gel, velvet, leather, Carrara marble or brushed steel. Procedural PBR finishes stay attached to the surface while GPU deformation bends the studio lighting into the finger's indentation. Memory foam recovers slowly after a short plateau, gel rebounds through rest, and rigid samples acknowledge contact with a small bounce and a primary-colour hairline ripple. The theme-aware Model Viewer showroom supplies HDR lighting, a reflective floor and contact shadows, complemented by a soft key shadow. Filter chips and empty-space flings switch samples with the app's spring; reduced motion and QA mode switch instantly. A polished ceramic SceneView cube opens the experience by loading the foam, without logo brackets. The `feel` and `matter` deep links, recording, lifecycle cancellation and existing haptic patterns remain supported. Physics, rounded geometry, tangent frames, picking, framing and caption resources have JVM tests. This demo adds no public SDK API.
