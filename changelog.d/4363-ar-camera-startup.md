- **Android AR: no black flash before the camera on re-entry ([#4363](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4363)).** Opaque AR views keep a neutral dark cover until a render pass containing a camera image has been submitted, and for two presented frames after it so a `TextureView` never shows black between the cover and the camera. Virtual content stays hidden under the cover too until the first camera image arrives — before, nodes were rendered over black when no camera image ever came. A `SurfaceView` scene brought back from the background is covered again when its surface is recreated, until ARCore delivers a new image (or for at most one second, so a finished `playbackDataset` that sends no new image is not covered forever), instead of showing its black new surface. The cover never goes up while the view is still on screen, so an exit transition keeps the last camera image. Timestamp-zero frames no longer reveal an uninitialised camera texture.
