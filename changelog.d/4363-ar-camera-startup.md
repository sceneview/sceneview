- **Android AR: no black flash before the camera on re-entry ([#4363](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4363)).** Opaque AR views keep a neutral dark cover until a render pass containing a camera image has been submitted, and for two presented frames after it so a `TextureView` never shows black between the cover and the camera. A `SurfaceView` scene brought back from the background is covered again until ARCore delivers a new image, instead of showing its recreated, black surface. Timestamp-zero frames no longer reveal an uninitialised camera texture.
