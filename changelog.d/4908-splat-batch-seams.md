- **`SplatNode` draws every splat of clouds over 32,767 splats.** Filament silently clamps hardware instancing to 32,767 instances per renderable, and `SplatNode` batched by 65,535, so the back half of every batch was never drawn: straight-edged gaps along the batch seams. Batches now match the clamp (`SplatBuffers.MAX_INSTANCES_PER_BATCH` is 32,767), and re-sorts reuse their arrays instead of allocating 12 bytes per splat. New `SplatNode.shownSplats` (also on the `SplatNode {}` composable) hides part of a cloud through its next sort, off the main thread, without building a new node. Demo (QA preview): the scan result's Photo layer draws its 65,535 splats whole, hides the faces turned away with one node instead of rebuilding it on the main thread, and is offered again when a scan is reopened from its cached model.
