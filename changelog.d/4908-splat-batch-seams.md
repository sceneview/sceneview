- **`SplatNode` draws every splat of clouds over 32,767 splats.** Filament silently clamps hardware instancing to 32,767 instances per renderable, and `SplatNode` batched by 65,535, so the back half of every batch was never drawn: straight-edged gaps along the batch seams. Batches now match the clamp (`SplatBuffers.MAX_INSTANCES_PER_BATCH` is 32,767), and re-sorts reuse their arrays instead of allocating 12 bytes per splat. Demo (QA preview): the scan result's Photo layer draws every surfel with no 65,535 cap, swaps faces off the main thread, and is offered again when a scan is reopened from its cached model.
