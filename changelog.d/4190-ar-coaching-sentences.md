- **AR coaching says what to do, in a full sentence ([#4190](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4190)).** `ARCoachingOverlay` now writes its instruction under the animated glyph — "Move your phone slowly over the floor or a table.", "Paused. Move your phone more slowly, in a brighter spot.", "Point your phone back at where you placed the object." — instead of one word ("Scan", "Paused", "Look back") that left first-time users guessing, as the 3D AR Model Viewer app and Apple's coaching overlay do. The sentence is still the accessible name, read once. In the Android demo, the same animated coaching now also guides Measure, Plane Lifecycle and Plane Renderer until the first surface is found (new `rememberSurfaceScanCue`); after a placement, "Drag to move. Pinch to resize. Twist to turn." stays 6 s instead of 3.5 s and leaves at the first gesture; the no-surface card suggests "a brighter area with a rug or visible floor detail"; and the placement screens no longer say "Tracking" to the user.
