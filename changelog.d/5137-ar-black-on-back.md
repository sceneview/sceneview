- **AR: the camera no longer turns black on Back with the default `lifecycle`.** Inside a `NavHost`, `ARSceneView` pauses its session when the exit transition starts; the next frame was still drawn over the camera texture ARCore had just released, so the scene went black for the whole transition. A paused session now keeps its last camera image on screen (the documented behaviour), and so does a view whose camera another `ARSceneView` took. The camera is still released on Back, Home and screen off. Pass `lifecycle = rememberHostLifecycle()` to keep the camera live during the transition (#5137).
