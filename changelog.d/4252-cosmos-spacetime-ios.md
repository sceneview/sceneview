- **iOS demo app: Cosmos Spacetime mode ([#4252](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4252)).** As on Android (#4251), the Star scene gets a Starlight | Spacetime pill above the dock. Spacetime flies the camera up and lays the system on a lit fabric sheet that every mass bends: the star sinks into a deep hollow whose rim casts a long teardrop shadow, the planets sit in their own dimples. `sceneview://demo/cosmos?tab=spacetime` opens straight on it.
