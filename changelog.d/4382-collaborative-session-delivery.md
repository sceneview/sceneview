- **Collaborative AR: a placement is no longer lost, and both devices agree on a node ([#4382](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4382)).** `CollaborativeSession` sent everything through one conflated queue, so a `placeNode`, the Cloud Anchor id or the `hello` could be replaced by the next line before it left the device. Only camera poses are conflated now. Inbound messages are merged one at a time, in arrival order, instead of concurrently. And calling `placeNode` on a key a peer placed first now shows on the caller's device too — it used to keep the peer's old copy while every other device got the new one. No API or wire-format change.
