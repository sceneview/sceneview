- **Android automatic instant placement ([#4858](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4858)).** Opt in with `AutoPlacementScene(instantPlacement = true)` to show horizontal content after 0.6 s of tracking before a plane is detected. `instantPlacementSize` supplies the displayed model size for the distance estimate; `AutoPlacementState.trackingMethod` exposes approximate/full tracking. A fully tracked instant point is re-anchored to the plane beneath it with a landing haptic. Dragging works on the estimated contact plane before a detected plane is available. Off by default; existing binary signatures, positional arguments and trailing-lambda calls are retained.

- **Instant-placement drags follow tracking refinement.** Pending estimated-floor moves retain their anchor-relative offset when ARCore refines the floor, so release can land on the corrected surface. Estimated-floor drag targets obey the same camera-distance limits as detected planes.
