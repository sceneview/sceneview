- **The default scene is anti-aliased for real, and dynamic resolution pays for it ([#3719](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/3719)).** An out-of-the-box `SceneView` shipped MSAA off and dynamic resolution off — a fixed 1× buffer with FXAA as the only anti-aliasing — and FXAA blurs a jagged edge rather than resolving it, so every default scene showed stair-stepping on its silhouettes. The default (and `RenderQuality.Default`, which must match it) now enables **4× MSAA on top of FXAA**, because the two solve different halves of the artifact: MSAA resolves the geometry edges FXAA can only blur, FXAA still covers the shading and alpha-tested edges MSAA never sees. That is paid for by handing Filament the authority to take resolution back where the asking would cost frames: **dynamic resolution is now on** — homogeneous, MEDIUM, and floored at `minScale = 0.75` rather than Filament's stock 0.5, because a quarter off each axis is inside what the FXAA pass can carry while a half is not, and a safety valve that produces a visibly broken frame is not a safety valve. `Renderer.frameRateOptions.scaleRate` goes from Filament's 1/15 to **1/8** so that valve reacts within a few frames of a spike instead of a quarter second; `headRoomRatio` and `history` are written down at Filament's own defaults so a future upstream change shows up as a diff here rather than as a silent behaviour change in every scene. `hdrColorBuffer` stays MEDIUM, and `RenderQuality.Cinematic` and `RenderQuality.Performance` are untouched. The preset is applied on **both** Filament objects it lives on: `view.applyRenderQuality(quality)` for the post-processing and `renderer.applyRenderQuality(quality)` for the controller tuning, both wired from `SceneView` and keyed on the `view` and `renderer` actually in use — so passing your own `Renderer` through `SceneView(renderer = …)` no longer gets you the `View` half of the preset paired with Filament's stock controller. `ARSceneView` does **not** inherit any of this: its `renderQuality` stays nullable and defaults to `null`, which means no preset, because `createARView` deliberately keeps MSAA, SSAO, bloom and dynamic resolution off the live camera feed — passing `RenderQuality.Default` to an AR view explicitly turns MSAA 4× and dynamic resolution on over that feed, which is documented on the parameter. Scenes that want the previous behaviour back — a fixed-resolution buffer with no MSAA — should pass `renderQuality = RenderQuality.Performance`, or set `view.multiSampleAntiAliasingOptions` and `view.dynamicResolutionOptions` themselves after the preset is applied.
