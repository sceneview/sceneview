- **The demo's camera card no longer sends to system settings for a dialog Android still shows, and the room dollhouse keeps its 3D view without the camera (Android demo)** — dismissing the camera dialog twice (Back, or a tap outside it) turned the card into "Open settings — Camera access is off for this app", although the permission was not fixed and asking again would have worked. A dismissal is now judged the same however many times it happens: the card keeps "Allow camera", and "Open settings" is left for a dialog Android has really stopped showing (a second "Don't allow", or a request answered with no dialog). The "answered with no dialog" threshold moves from 500 ms to the library's one second — a real dialog came back in 509 ms on a Pixel 4a — and the card says why the demo needs the camera once it has been refused. "Your room, as a dollhouse" is no longer held behind the camera permission as a whole: its 3D view, its list of recordings and its empty state open with the camera refused, and only its AR view asks (#4460)
