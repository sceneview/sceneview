- **Demo app: a shared Room Scan opens in any 3D viewer ([#4334](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4334)).** The model you share from a scan's replay is now a lighter copy — about 100,000 triangles, resting on its floor — so AR Model Viewer and other viewers open it. The first share takes a few seconds to prepare it; the surface on screen keeps its full detail. On a phone too short of memory to prepare it, the full model is shared instead, and the replay says so.
