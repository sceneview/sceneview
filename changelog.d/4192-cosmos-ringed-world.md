- **Demo app: Cosmos' Star scene gains a ringed world ([#4192](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4192)).** A planet with translucent rings now orbits the blue star, lit by it: a soft day/night terminator, an atmosphere rim that glows when the star is behind it, the rings' shadow across its bands and the planet's shadow across the rings, and a faint orbit trail. The scene opens on the whole system; tap the planet (or the star) and the camera flies there on the design system's `ease-expressive` curve, round the star rather than through it. Two small unlit materials (`cosmos_planet`, `cosmos_ring`) compute the light themselves, so there is no Filament light or shadow map to pay for. Android only for now; the iOS twin follows.
