- **`DESIGN.md` now prescribes Material 3 and Apple HIG instead of a house look ([#4941](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4941)).** Material 3 on Android and the web, Apple HIG on Apple platforms, WCAG 2.2 AA, the system font, and M3 colour roles generated from the single source colour `#005BC1` (M3 Fidelity scheme, light and dark, so the brand blue stays the primary container). It adds an explicit Forbidden list (gradients, glass, custom fonts, decorative pills and callouts, shadowed cards), a licence and quality rule for the 3D models shown in captures and stores, and one review test: "could this be a Google or Apple screen?". The `CinematicHero` recipe in `llms.txt` now uses a flat `surfaceContainerLow` backdrop instead of a gradient.
