- **Demo app: the Rerun scan ends with a real 3D model of your room.** While you record, the demo fuses ARCore's raw depth into a colour volume (a TSDF: 2.5 cm voxels, 48 MB at most). A new **Build 3D model** button in the replay turns it into one solid, coloured mesh (marching cubes, stray fragments removed), off the main thread with a progress bar. You can orbit the model and share it as a `.glb` that Blender, three.js or any glTF viewer opens at real size. A scan reopened later builds its model from its saved surfaces. The whole pipeline is plain Kotlin, tested on the JVM against shapes whose surface is known.
