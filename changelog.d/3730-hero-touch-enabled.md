- **`SceneView(isTouchEnabled:)` — a scene can now decline touch entirely ([#3730](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/3730)).** A `SceneView` installed a touch listener unconditionally and reported **every** event handled, even with `cameraManipulator = null`, `onGestureListener = null` and `onTouchEvent = null` — so a scene placed under scrollable content silently swallowed its parent's scroll: a drag that began over the 3D view never reached the `LazyColumn`, the `HorizontalPager` or the parent `scrollable` arbitrating above it, and the page would not move. Nothing a caller could pass made it give the gesture back. It also ray-cast the scene on each event to produce a hit result nobody had asked for. `isTouchEnabled = false` installs **no listener at all**: the view consumes nothing for the whole gesture, and casts no collision ray per event. That is the setting for a scene used as a passive backdrop — a home-screen hero, a decorative header, a thumbnail. It is applied on update as well as at attach, so a scene may become interactive after it was composed.

- **Android demo — the home hero's camera is driven by the page scroll ([#3730](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/3730)).** The top card used to be an orbit-manipulated `SceneView` inside the scrolling grid, which meant a thumb starting on it moved nothing, and the only camera motion on offer — pinch zoom — read as almost nothing at card size. The hero is now a pinned stage drawn *under* the catalogue: a fixed 420 dp rectangle that never moves and never resizes, **clipped** down to a permanent 80 dp band as the page scrolls, with the camera trajectory playing out behind that clip. The single scroll progress that drives it is read at draw time, so the choreography costs no recomposition; the band hands the drags it receives back to the grid rather than eating them, and a tap on it opens the model viewer. With Reduce Motion on there is no trajectory — two poses, and a subject that neither turns nor dollies. The stage renders on demand and draws nothing at rest.
