Physics

A chaotic double pendulum, the same simulation as on Android and iOS.

The integrator mirrors the shared DoublePendulum simulation in sceneview-core (Kotlin Multiplatform). Adapted from radcli14's twolinks.

Spatial audio

A bell orbits the scene. Headphones recommended.

A looping bell plays as a SpatialAudioNode: it pans between your ears and fades with distance, through a Web Audio PannerNode in HRTF mode. The listener follows the orbit camera, as in the Android and iOS demos.
