A 3D file lands in Messages, Mail or Files. Tap it, and it is standing on your desk at its real size.

SceneView opens USDZ, Reality, STL, OBJ, PLY and 3MF — including the mesh formats no stock app on your iPhone will open — straight from the share sheet, with no conversion step and no account.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Open a 3D file from anywhere — Files, Mail, Messages or any app's share sheet.
- See it at true scale — point at a flat surface, tap to place, then drag, pinch and twist it into position.
- Trust the size — a printable part arrives in real-world units and the right way up, so what sits on your table is what would come off the printer.
- Let the real room win — real furniture and passing people stay in front of virtual objects, and the light around you lights the model.
- Hang it on a wall, or place it instantly without waiting for a plane.
- Change the light — swap HDR environments and watch materials, reflections and shadows answer in real time.
- Browse Sketchfab from inside the app and open what you find in the viewer.
- Wander 48 demo screens — placement, image and face tracking, scene mesh, depth, physics, materials, geometry, spatial audio and more.

FOR DEVELOPERS

This app is the live showcase for SceneView, the open-source 3D and AR SDK for SwiftUI. A model on screen is one view; AR placement is one call. Every one of the 48 screens ships its source, and each links to the code that draws it.

github.com/sceneview/sceneview

PRIVACY

No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads. Your models and the camera feed stay on your device — the camera is used only for AR, and nothing it sees is stored or uploaded. The one feature that reaches the network is the online model gallery, and only while you are searching it.
