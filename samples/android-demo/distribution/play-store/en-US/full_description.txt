See what the SceneView SDK can do, on your own phone, before you write a line of code.

SceneView is an open-source 3D and augmented reality SDK for Jetpack Compose, SwiftUI, and the web. This app is its demo: 48 screens, each one a working sample of a feature you can put in your own app. Every screen links to the source code that draws it, and most of them are only a few lines long.

WHAT THE SAMPLES SHOW

- 3D models — load GLB and glTF files, play their animations, and orbit, pinch, and zoom the camera.
- Augmented reality — place a model on a table, hang something flat against a wall, track images and faces, and measure a real distance.
- Real-world blending — real furniture and people stay in front of virtual objects, and the room's own light lights the model.
- Lighting and materials — swap HDR environments and watch reflections and shadows change in real time.
- Anchors, depth, physics, geometry, spatial audio, radiance fields, and more.
- Your own files — open a GLB, glTF, OBJ, STL, PLY, or 3MF file and see how the SDK renders it.

FOR DEVELOPERS

A 3D model on screen is one composable, and AR placement is one call. Find the screen that looks like what you want to build, open its code, and copy it into your project. The SDK, the samples, and this app are free and open source.

github.com/sceneview/sceneview

PRIVACY

No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads. Your models and the camera feed are processed on your device — the camera is used only for AR, and nothing it sees is stored or uploaded. Two things reach the network, and only when you ask for them: searching the online model galleries, and the handful of samples that host an anchor with Google's ARCore cloud service.
