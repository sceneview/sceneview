- New: Ask in AR (beta). Ask a question out loud and the answer is drawn in your room, in handwriting, to its real measurements.
- "Where should I hang this picture?" A level line, two nails, then the frame on the wall.
- "Will a 2 m sofa fit here?" Its footprint on the floor, then the sofa itself, to scale.
- "How do I bleed this radiator?" A circle round the screw, the key turning a quarter turn, and the steps.
