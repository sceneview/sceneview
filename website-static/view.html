Open a 3D model from a link

Paste the https link of a .glb, .gltf or .3mf file. It opens here in 3D, and in AR on your phone.

Your browser loads the file straight from where it is hosted. Nothing is uploaded to SceneView.

Share or generate a link https://sceneview.github.io/view?url=<encoded https link to a .glb, .gltf or .3mf> Add &usdz=<link to a .usdz> to open AR on iPhone and iPad. The file's host must allow cross-origin requests (CORS).
