# Privacy Policy

The full, current privacy policy is published at
**[sceneview.github.io/privacy.html](https://sceneview.github.io/privacy.html)**. It is the
only version the app stores link to; this page is a summary.

- **SceneView SDK** — collects nothing. The library has no analytics or crash-reporting
  dependency and no server of its own. An app built with SceneView is covered by that app's
  own privacy policy. To measure your own app, see
  [Measure your AR funnel](recipes/measure-ar-funnel.md).
- **SceneView Demo app** (Android and iOS) — uses Google Firebase: Analytics for usage
  events, Crashlytics for crash reports, and Cloud Messaging for notifications you allow.
  No accounts, no ads, no advertising identifier, no cross-app tracking. Analytics and
  crash reporting can be switched off in the app's Settings; notifications in Settings or
  through the system permission. On Android, the Cloud Anchors and Geospatial demos rely on
  Google ARCore cloud services, which process camera and location data to power those
  sessions.
- **This website** — Google Analytics 4 page-view analytics, skipped entirely when the
  browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
